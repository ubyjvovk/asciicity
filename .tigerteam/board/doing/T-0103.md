## Goal
Two west-Tokyo presets on the v2 dataset — `shibuya` (the Scramble
Crossing outside Shibuya Station's Hachiko exit; the world-famous one) and
`shinjuku` (a big crossing by Shinjuku Station's east exit / Kabukicho) —
and the Tokyo registry entry's `defaultSpawn` changed to `shibuya`, so
entering Tokyo starts at the Scramble. User decision 2026-08-31.

## Context
- **Read first**: the T-0098 preset derivation pattern in
  `src/data/spawn.ts` (Tokyo block comments) and its tests in
  `tests/spawn.test.ts` — copy the discipline exactly: coordinates DERIVED
  from the committed dataset (road vertices from tile files, anchors from
  `index.landmarks`/`places`), never hand-typed; walkability proven via a
  CollisionGrid built from the preset's 3×3 committed tiles.
- T-0102's report names the landmark/place entries it found near the two
  stations — start from those anchors.
- `shibuya`: pick a road vertex ON or immediately adjacent to the scramble
  intersection north of Shibuya Station (the Hachiko-square crossing),
  bearing looking ACROSS the crossing into the building wall of
  center-gai (north-west-ish) — this is a street-canyon vantage, not a
  tower vantage, so no sightline-to-anchor test; instead assert the
  vertex is within 120 m of the Shibuya Station anchor/place and passes
  walkability with 6 m clearance relaxed to whatever the crossing's road
  density allows (state the clearance you achieve; ≥ 2 m required).
- `shinjuku`: a road vertex within 150 m of Shinjuku Station's east side,
  toward Kabukicho (north-east of the station), same rules.
- Both presets: `city: 'tokyo'`, labels in the existing style. Update the
  Tokyo registry blurb if it reads oddly with the new spawn (keep it one
  short line; mention Shibuya).
- `e2e/tokyo.spec.ts`: add one boot test — plain `?city=tokyo` (no `at`)
  reaches `ready` with the WORLD row within 60 m of the shibuya preset's
  local coordinates (the default-spawn proof), tiles loaded non-empty.
- The wave-11 presets and their tests must remain untouched and green.
