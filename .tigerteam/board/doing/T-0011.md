**For the reviewer — one deliberate deviation from the Context spec:** the ticket said to sample the non-black fraction *every 8th pixel* and expect `> 0.02`. With the shipped renderer that literal grid under-reports badly (≈1.2%) because the ASCII glyphs are thin (1–2 px strokes inside 6×12 px cells): a coarse fixed-step sample starting at (0,0) aliases systematically into the gaps between strokes, and the test only passes if the sample resolves the strokes (step ≤ 2). I switched to sampling **every pixel** — deterministic at ≈3.9% across repeated runs on the synthetic city — keeping the `> 0.02` threshold and the "prove it's not black" intent. The change is documented in the spec comment and `docs/testing.md`. If the PM prefers keeping a coarser sample, the threshold/step would need recalibration against the real glyph coverage.
