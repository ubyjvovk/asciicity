## Goal

Make Sydney's landmarks recognisable and reachable: curated landmark
fixes (colours/labels/heights where OSM stubs them), twelve spawn presets
with mechanically-tested vantages, and a default Circular Quay spawn that
frames the Opera House across the cove.

## Context

- Patterns: `LANDMARK_FIXES` / `EXTRA_BUILDINGS` in
  `src/world/landmarks.ts` (see the `sf` and `nyc` entries and their
  comments); presets in `src/data/spawn.ts` (see the tokyo block —
  T-0110 already added `'sydney'` to the city union and a placeholder
  `circularquay`). Read T-0110's Worker report
  (`.tigerteam/board/done/T-0110.md`) for the Opera House / Sydney Tower
  massing findings before deciding which fixes are needed.
- **Fixes are conditional on data** (verify against the fetched tiles,
  fix only what is actually stubbed — the SF comment style documents each
  check):
  - `Sydney Opera House` (building relation): colour ivory `0xf5f0e6`;
    if the outline height is a stub (< 5 m), set `h: 10` — PODIUM height
    only. Do NOT fake the sails' height on the outline: the shells are a
    synthesised structure arriving in T-0114 (§4.22), which sits on top
    of this podium. Label stays the OSM name.
  - `St Mary's Cathedral`: if h < 20, curated `h: 75`, `shape: 'spire'`,
    colour sandstone `0xcbb69a`.
  - `Luna Park Sydney` (or the actual OSM name): `label: 'Luna Park'`.
  - Fort Denison's building (whatever OSM names it): `label:
    'Fort Denison'`, ivory.
  - `Sydney Tower` (a `building:part`, h ≈ 270) and `Crown Sydney`
    (h ≈ 271): verify they render from OSM data; fix ONLY if stubbed.
    Report actual heights.
- **Presets** (12, replace the placeholder `circularquay`; keys and
  intents locked, exact coordinates/bearings are yours to derive from the
  data): `circularquay` (default spawn: western quay promenade near
  151.2100, -33.8580, bearing ≈ 75° — the Opera House across the cove),
  `operahouse` (building preset, exact name `Sydney Opera House`),
  `harbourbridge` (coordinate preset ON the eastern walkway deck near
  mid-span, ≈ 151.2111, -33.8503, facing the CBD skyline southwards),
  `mrsmacquarie` (Mrs Macquarie's Point, ≈ 151.2222, -33.8587, bearing ≈
  290° — the postcard: Opera House with the bridge behind), `lunapark`
  (Milsons Point boardwalk, facing the harbour/CBD), `therocks`,
  `barangaroo`, `darlingharbour`, `botanicgarden`, `kingscross`,
  `centralstation`, `northsydney`.
- Vantage rules (locked lessons — do not substitute zone-text checks):
  every preset passes the clear-corridor rule
  (`blocked(pt + k·forward, 1.5) === false` for k = 4…40 m, T-0059
  pattern); the four view presets (`circularquay`, `operahouse`,
  `mrsmacquarie`, `harbourbridge`, `lunapark`) additionally pass a
  buildings-only sightline test to their subject (ray sampled every 10 m,
  `blocked(pt, 2) === false`, T-0098 pattern; for `mrsmacquarie` the
  subject is the Opera House anchor). Water is NOT an obstacle in the
  buildings-only grid — sightlines across the cove are the point.
- The Harbour Bridge deck humps landed in T-0112 (`deckApexASL` 49), so a
  mid-span `harbourbridge` spawn stands at ≈ 45–50 m ASL.
- e2e budget (CI is slow on I/O — T-0101): extend `e2e/sydney.spec.ts` by
  at most two specs: `?at=harbourbridge` boots with player y > 35, and
  `?at=mrsmacquarie` boots with a non-empty LANDMARK/ZONE row. Use the
  memoized helpers in unit tests.
