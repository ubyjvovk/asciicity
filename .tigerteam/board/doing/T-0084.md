## Goal
`buildSuspensionBridge` builds the deck box per East-Sidewalk polyline
segment with a FLAT top at the segment's mid-height (`bridge.ts` ≈ line
554: `top = deckY(frame, sMid)`). OSM crosses the whole main span in one
884 m segment (z −3255 → −4139) over which the deck rises ~5 m, so the flat
top is up to 2.6 m above the sloped road/sidewalk ribbons and hides them
for the south half of the span (PM GPU frames from the East Sidewalk and
from 90 m above: solid orange deck, no road; from the West Sidewalk the
roadway shows). Make the box follow the profile. Contract:
`docs/architecture.md` §4.16 "Deck box … The top follows the deck profile
(T-0084)" — read it first.

## Context
- In the deck-box loop: subdivide every polyline segment into pieces of
  ≤ 50 m; for each piece sample `deckY` at BOTH ends (`s0`, `s1`) and build
  a sloped prism: top edge from `y0 − 0.4` to `y1 − 0.4`, bottom edge from
  `y0 − 8.0` to `y1 − 8.0`, width `2·halfW`, six faces with outward normals
  (the existing `box` helper takes one top/bottom pair — add a `prism`
  helper or extend `box` with per-end heights; keep `beam` untouched).
- Hangers already sample `deckY` per position; cables, towers, anchorages
  unchanged. Vertex budget stays < 25 000 (≈ 55 extra pieces × 24 verts).
- `tests/bridge.test.ts` — keep all existing cases, add:
  - `'deck box top tracks the deck profile within 0.1 m every 25 m along the east sidewalk'`
    (on `sf.json` with `Terrain` + `BridgeDecks` + `makeGroundAt` built
    exactly as `src/main.ts` does — `chainBridgeRoads` is already inside
    `BridgeDecks`; walk the chained East Sidewalk (`chainBridgeRoads` from
    `src/world/terrain.ts`) every 25 m, find the highest bridge vertex
    within 3 m laterally of the sidewalk line and within 30 m below the
    walking surface, and assert it equals `groundAt − 0.4 ± 0.1`)
  - `'no deck-box piece is longer than 50 m'`
  Update the vertex-count assertion if needed and report the new count.
