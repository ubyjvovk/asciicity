## Goal
Turn building footprints into one merged, lit, vertex-coloured mesh with
window-textured walls and flat roofs — the dominant visual of the game.

## Context
- Contract: `docs/architecture.md` §4.2 (walls/roofs/uv/normals/winding/
  groups/materials — implement exactly), §4.3 (palette, exact hex values and
  `colorFor`), §4.4 (`makeWindowTexture`, browser-only, seeded). Use the
  PM-owned `MeshBuilder`/`toGeometry` from `src/world/mesh.ts` (read it first).
- Exports: `buildBuildingsMesh(buildings: Building[]): MeshData`,
  `makeBuildingsObject(buildings: Building[], windowTex: THREE.Texture): THREE.Mesh`,
  `PALETTE`, `LANDMARK_PALETTE`, `colorFor`, `makeWindowTexture`.
  Also export the helper `normalizeRing(poly: Vec2[]): Vec2[]` (returns a copy
  with positive `ShapeUtils.area`).
- `makeWindowTexture` may only be called in a browser; keep it in
  `textures.ts` with no top-level side effects so importing the module in
  node is safe (`buildings.ts` must not import `textures.ts`).
- `tests/mesh.test.ts` covers the PM-owned `MeshBuilder` (see criteria).
- Write `docs/world.md`: how a footprint becomes geometry (with the uv scale
  and group layout), the palette, and the texture.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `bash .tigerteam/scripts/run-tests.sh tests/buildings.test.ts tests/mesh.test.ts` exits 0
- [ ] `tests/buildings.test.ts` covers: a 10×10 square footprint at `h=5` yields 4 walls (24 vertices) + 2 roof triangles (6 vertices), groups `[ {start:0,count:24,materialIndex:0}, {start:24,count:6,materialIndex:1} ]`; every wall normal is unit length, horizontal, and points away from the centroid (`dot(n, mid - centroid) > 0`); every wall triangle's geometric normal `cross(b-a, c-a)` has positive dot with its stored normal; roof normals are `(0,1,0)` and roof triangles wind upward; wall uvs — `u` runs 0 → 40/24 around the ring, `v` is 0 at the base and `5/24` at the top; a clockwise input ring gives identical geometry to its reversed copy (`normalizeRing`); rings with `|area| < 1` produce no vertices; vertex colours equal the linear rgb of `colorFor` (`new THREE.Color(hex)`); `colorFor` picks `LANDMARK_PALETTE[id % 4]` for named buildings and `PALETTE[id % 8]` otherwise
