## Goal
With T-0159 every building mesh can carry an `extra` vec4 (OSM facade or
roof colour in linear rgb, −1 when absent; material code in w). Make the
cyberpunk facades use it: real facade colours darkened for night and a
material-specific surface pattern — brick courses, stone ashlar, glass
curtain walls, metal standing seams, wood boards, plaster. Contract:
docs/architecture.md §4.11 "cyberpunk v2" → "Facades × OSM (wave 21,
T-0161, extends T-0152)" and "Facades".

## Context
- Builds on T-0152's facade (read its doc `docs/styles/cyberpunk-facades.md`
  and `facademath.ts` first; keep all T-0152 tests green).
- Read the attribute with `attribute('extra', 'vec4')` from 'three/tsl'. A
  material that reads a missing attribute must never be put on a mesh
  without it, so build TWO variants in `makeFacadeMaterials`: `walls` /
  `roof` (today's, attribute-free) and `wallsOsm` / `roofOsm` (reading
  `extra`, falling back to the procedural look per vertex when rgb < 0).
  Extend `FacadeMaterials` with the two new fields. The PM wires `wet.ts`
  on accept (`geometry.hasAttribute('extra') ? [wallsOsm, roofOsm] : [walls, roof]`);
  to test your variants before that, a throwaway local edit of wet.ts is
  fine but must NOT be committed.
- Material codes (from `MATERIAL_CODE` in `world/buildings.ts`): 0 none, 1 brick,
  2 stone, 3 concrete, 4 glass, 5 metal, 6 wood, 7 plaster.
- Pure mirrors in facademath.ts: `nightAlbedo(rgb)`, the per-material
  pattern parameters table, and `windowLight` with a glass multiplier
  (offices: lit ×1.5, clamp ≤ 0.3).
