## Goal

Put Sydney's ferries on the harbour: a third instanced ship class with the
green-and-cream Sydney ferry silhouette, running five Circular Quay routes
(one passing under the Harbour Bridge) plus a sailboat lane, with night
lights — the harbour is currently empty water.

## Context

- **The contract is `docs/architecture.md` §4.17b** — hull dimensions and
  colours, `FERRY_SPEED_MPS`, lights, the six lanes with counts, and the
  water-sample rule. Read it and §4.17 first.
- Pattern: `src/world/ships.ts` — copy the cargo/sail structure exactly
  (`buildFerryHull`/`buildFerryLights`, `ferryWalkers`, one hull + one
  lights `InstancedMesh`, a `writeClass` call). Extend
  `ShipLane.kind` to `'cargo' | 'sail' | 'ferry'`. `ShipFleet` and
  `main.ts` are already city-generic — `SHIP_LANES.sydney` is all the
  wiring there is; do not touch `main.ts`.
- The ferry is double-ended (§4.17b): build it fore-aft symmetric so
  `reverseAtEnds` needs no flip.
- Starting waypoints (route intent — nudge freely to satisfy the water
  test, keep each route's character):
  - Manly reach (count 2): `[151.2119,-33.8585] [151.2145,-33.8545]
    [151.2200,-33.8515] [151.2280,-33.8480] [151.2440,-33.8430]`

    [151.2230,-33.8500] [151.2350,-33.8450]`
  - Parramatta River service, UNDER the bridge, north of Goat Island

    [151.2095,-33.8510] [151.2040,-33.8480] [151.1950,-33.8450]
