## Goal

Give the Sydney Harbour Bridge its real silhouette: a synthesised
two-rib steel through-arch (chords, truss verticals, hangers/struts, deck
box, four granite pylons) built from a spec table, exactly as the Golden
Gate got towers and cables in wave 9. Without it the bridge is a bare road
over water — the wave-9 user feedback ("just a plain road — real
geometry") applies squared to this bridge.

## Context

- **The contract is `docs/architecture.md` §4.16b** — `ArchBridgeSpec`,
  `ARCH_BRIDGES`, `buildArchBridge`, `archAnchors`, the geometry rules and
  numeric table values (archTopASL 134, archBottomASL 116, springASL 12,
  deckApexASL 49, ribSep 30, pylonTopASL 89, colours). Read it first.
- Follow the suspension builder in `src/world/bridge.ts` as the pattern:
  same MeshBuilder/prism style, same frame math, reuse its humped-deck
  helper for deck level along the axis (§4.16b requires it).
- Spec binding for the sydney entry:
  `sidewalks: ['Bradfield Highway']` (one-walkway mode, the motorway is the
  deck axis), `deckWidth: 49`,
  `deckRoads: ['Harbour Bridge Cycleway', 'Cahill Walk']`.
  T-0110's Worker report confirms these names and piece counts in
  `index.bridgeRoads` — read it (`.tigerteam/board/done/T-0110.md`).
- `ends` initial values: south `[151.21070, -33.85245]`, north
  `[151.21164, -33.84792]`. The north pair comes from OSM `bridge:support`
  footprints (ways 1238822733/34); the south value is a PM estimate and
  MUST be reconciled against the dataset: prefer a south
  `bridge:support`/pylon footprint pair if T-0110's report found one, else
  place the south end ON the chained `Bradfield Highway` bridge polyline
  503 m from the north end. Report the delta from the ticket's initial
  values (wave-10 lesson: PM spec coordinates are hypotheses, the data is
  the truth).
- `deckHumps` currently iterates `SUSPENSION_BRIDGES` only; make it (and
  `makeBridgesObject`) iterate both tables with unchanged signatures.
  `main.ts` anchor arrays (initial build AND `rebuildFromTiles`) get an
  `archAnchors` flatMap beside the existing `bridgeAnchors` ones.
- Known data quirk to observe, not fix: `Cahill Walk` also names the
  elevated walkway along Circular Quay south of the bridge. If chaining
  (§4.9) merges it with the harbour-crossing pieces, the single hump may
  visibly distort the Circular Quay stretch. Report what the chain looks
  like (piece count, endpoint span) and how the hump lands; do NOT patch
  data or chaining.

## Rework (attempt 2)

The code, tests and both reconciliations were executed exactly as
instructed — the fault is the ticket's anchor hint and an
under-constrained criterion, so this rework replaces both. What the GPU
review found: the arch stands ~400 m too far north — its south springing
rises out of open water mid-harbour and its northern half covers Milsons
Point streets (PM probe from the reconciled mid-span (62, −1251): ALT 5 m
ASL, ZONE OLYMPIC DRIVE, the player stands on land UNDER the lattice).
Root cause: the OSM `bridge:support` pair at z ≈ −1479 is the NORTHERN
APPROACH VIADUCT's support, not the main pylons — "within 20 m of the
chained polyline" was satisfiable anywhere along the 1.6 km chain, so
nothing caught it.

**New mechanical rule for `ends` (replaces the support-pair/503 m rule):**

1. Chain the `Bradfield Highway` bridge pieces; take the longest chain.
2. Walk it and find its two crossings with the boundary of the LARGEST
   water ring by polygon area — NOT the odd-parity test: the shipped
   dataset currently contains two near-duplicate giant harbour rings
   (12.6 and 13.3 km², a coastline-closure bug being handled in the
   T-0116 line of work), and odd-parity calls their overlap land. The
   largest-ring rule stays correct before and after that data fix.
   PM probe (this dataset, both giant rings agree): land→water at local
   (79.7, −1196.6) [north shore], water→land at (−133.8, −788.4)
   [south shore]; water gap 461 m.
3. `ends` = those two crossing points, each extended 21 m LAND-ward
   along the local deck direction (springings sit just onshore).
   Expected values (assert within 25 m): south `[151.20945, -33.85434]`,
   north `[151.21197, -33.85031]`; the resulting span must be 503 ± 15 m.
   (Sanity: their midpoint is within ~20 m of the real bridge centre
   −33.85225, 151.21058.)

**Test changes** (`tests/arch.test.ts`):
- Replace the "ends within 20 m of the polyline" test with: each end
