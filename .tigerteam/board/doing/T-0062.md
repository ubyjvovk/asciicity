## Goal
Make the key landmarks recognisable from across the river: per
`docs/architecture.md` §4.13 "Shapes", a `shape` in the landmark table
(`dome` / `spire` / `tower`) becomes a procedural cap on the building.

## Context
- `Building.shape?` already exists in `src/data/types.ts` (PM-owned).
  `applyLandmarks` (T-0059) must copy `shape` from the fix onto the building
  (and the extra Motherland Monument gets `tower`); extend `LANDMARK_FIXES`
  with the shapes listed in §4.13 for Kyiv and London.
- `buildBuildingsMesh`: after emitting a building's roof, emit its cap in
  **group 0** (walls material) with the wall colour, per the exact geometry
  in §4.13 (dome: 8 segments × 4 rings hemisphere; spire: 8-segment cone;
  tower: half-size box of `0.5·h`). Normals outward (compute per face), UV
  `(0,0)`. Buildings without `shape` are byte-identical to today.
- Tests: `tests/buildings.test.ts` — a `dome` building adds exactly
  `8·4·2 = 64` triangles (192 vertices) in group 0 above roof height with
  every normal unit-length and pointing away from the dome centre; a
  `spire` adds 8 side triangles + 8 base triangles (or none — say which)
  reaching `roofY + 0.6·h` at the apex; `tower` adds a box whose top is
  `roofY + 0.5·h`; no `shape` → identical output to before (compare arrays).
  `tests/landmarks.test.ts` — Kyiv fixes carry the §4.13 shapes; Motherland
  extra is `tower`.
- `docs/world.md`: shapes section.
