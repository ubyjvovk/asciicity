## Goal
User feedback after playing: gloom is too washed out — the grey sky is right,
but roads and buildings should be darker with a bit more of their own colour.

## Context
- `src/render/ascii.ts` (T-0033): change ONLY the `washed` line, identically
  in the GLSL fragment shader and the TS mirror `gloomMix`:
  `vec3 washed = mix(vec3(lumT), tint, 0.55) * 0.26;`
  (was `mix(vec3(lumT), tint, 0.35) * 0.35` — retention 0.35→0.55, level
  0.35→0.26). `gloomBg` stays `vec3(0.72, 0.73, 0.75)`.
- `tests/ascii.test.ts`: update the white case to `[0.26, 0.26, 0.26]` and
  add a colour-retention case: `gloomMix([1, 0, 0], 1, 1)` → r component
  `(0.299 + 0.701·0.55)·0.26 ≈ 0.178` (±1e-3) and `r > 2·g`.
- `docs/render.md`: update the quoted shader block and the constant table.
- Flag for the PM: `docs/architecture.md` §4.8 shader needs the same one-line
  sync after accept (PM-owned, out of scope).
