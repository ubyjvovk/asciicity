## Rework (attempt 2)

Thanks for flagging the rain direction — you were right, and the GPU frame showed two more problems with my spec, so §4.11 "matrix" is revised (2026-08-27; read it again). All three are spec fixes, not implementation faults:

**What failed (PM GPU frame, `/?city=kyiv&at=maidan&render=matrix`):**
1. Rain rises (your finding).
2. The `0.35` floor lights every cell including the sky, so the frame is a uniform wall of dim glyphs — buildings and sky are indistinguishable.
3. Trails are invisible: `0.4 + 0.6·trail³` barely modulates, and every cell re-rolls its glyph 8×/s at the same instant, which reads as noise rather than rain.

**What done looks like** (all per the revised §4.11):
- `trail = fract(phase − time·speed·0.25 − vUv.y)`; `I = pow(trail, 4)`; head when `trail > 0.96`.
- colour `= (0.2, 1.0, 0.3)·mask·(S·(0.3 + 0.7·I) + 0.12·I)` with `S = shaped(bright(scene))`; head `(0.9, 1.0, 0.9)·mask·(0.4 + 0.6·S)`.
- glyph churn `floor(time·2 + 7·hash(cell.x, cell.y, 0))` window (≈ twice a second, per-cell phase).
- new pure `matrixBrightness(S, I, head)` mirrored by the shader; `rainIntensity` returns `I`; tests updated: head argmax moves toward `y01 = 0` over time; `matrixBrightness(0, 0, false)` is black; `matrixBrightness(0, 1, false)` is dim green (g ≈ 0.12); `matrixBrightness(1, 1, false)` is full green; head with S = 1 is near-white; `matrixGlyph` changes for a cell across a 0.5-s boundary and is constant within 0.1 s.
- headless evidence: on `/?synthetic=1&render=matrix&cell=3x6` the non-black fraction must be **between 5 % and 25 %** (it was 29 % with the uniform floor; the sky must go mostly dark) — quote the number.
- `bash scripts/check.sh` green; `docs/styles/matrix.md` updated.
