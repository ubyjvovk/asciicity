## Goal
Keep the player out of buildings: a spatial hash of footprints, point-in-
polygon and edge-distance tests, and a `resolve` that slides along walls
instead of stopping dead.

## Context
- Contract: `docs/architecture.md` §4.6 (exports, cell size, `blocked`,
  `resolve` order of fallbacks). Types from `src/data/types.ts`.
- Grid: `cellOf(v) = Math.floor(v / cell)`; a footprint is inserted into every
  cell its axis-aligned bounding box (expanded by 1 m) touches. `blocked`
  queries the cell of `p` plus the 8 neighbours.
- Pure module: no three.js imports needed.
- Write `docs/collision.md` (algorithm, radius, complexity).
