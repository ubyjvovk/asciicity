## Goal
The Golden Gate Bridge East Sidewalk arrives from OSM as three `bridge:
true` road pieces whose joints are over open water; `BridgeDecks` profiles
each piece between its own endpoints, so `groundAt` along that sidewalk is
−22 m (sea bed) for two-thirds of the span while the single-piece West
Sidewalk is a proper 35–45 m deck. Chain same-name bridge pieces into one
polyline before profiling. Contract: `docs/architecture.md` §4.9 "Bridge
chaining (wave 9, T-0082)" — read it first; it is the spec.

## Context
- `src/world/terrain.ts` `BridgeDecks` constructor (≈ line 205): before the
  `for (const road of roads)` loop, replace `roads` with
  `chainBridgeRoads(roads)`; everything downstream (per-segment buckets,
  `deckAt`, `makeGroundAt`) is unchanged.
- NEW export `chainBridgeRoads(roads: Road[]): Road[]` (pure): non-bridge
  roads and unnamed bridge roads pass through untouched; bridge roads with
  the same non-empty `name` are chained on coincident endpoints (distance
  ≤ 0.5 m), attaching after or before the growing chain and reversing a
  piece when its far end matches; loop until no piece attaches;
  independent of input order. The chained road keeps the first piece's
  `id`, `cls`, `name`, `bridge` and drops the duplicated joint vertex.
  Pieces that never join stay as they are.
- Measured on `public/data/sf.json` (PM, groundAt along the East Sidewalk
  vertices): z −2231 → 29.7, z −2427 → −14.9, z −2870 → −22, z −3255 →
  −28.4, z −4677 → 34.8, z −4903 → 45.6. After chaining, the East Sidewalk
  deck must run ~30 → ~46 m from abutment to abutment (≈ 37 at mid-span).
- `tests/terrain.test.ts` — keep every existing case, add:
  - `'chainBridgeRoads joins three same-name bridge pieces given in shuffled order, reversing one'`
    (fixture: pieces A, B(reversed), C; expect one road with A.start …
    C.end and no duplicated joints)
  - `'chainBridgeRoads leaves unnamed, differently named and non-bridge roads alone'`
  - `'chained deck: a three-piece bridge whose joints lie below the water is profiled between the outer abutments'`
    (synthetic `HeightFn`: abutments at 30 and 46, water at −24 in between;
    `deckAt` mid-span ≈ 38 ± 1)
  - `'sf.json: groundAt along the Golden Gate Bridge East Sidewalk never drops below 25 m and is within 4 m of the West Sidewalk'`
    (load `public/data/sf.json` like `tests/landmarks.test.ts`; build
    `Terrain` + `BridgeDecks` + `makeGroundAt` exactly as `src/main.ts`
    does; for each East Sidewalk vertex compare with `groundAt` at the
    nearest West Sidewalk point)
