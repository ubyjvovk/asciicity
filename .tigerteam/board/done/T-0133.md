## Goal
Every Minas Tirith building — houses, walls and the White Tower — currently
wears the office-window texture, so the white city reads as a business
district. Give cities a `facade` and ship a running-bond stone texture for
Minas Tirith, exactly per `docs/architecture.md` §4.23 "Facade (wave 16b,
T-0133)".

## Context
- `src/data/cities.ts`: `CityInfo.facade?: 'windows' | 'stone'` with JSDoc;
  set `facade: 'stone'` on the `minas-tirith` entry only.
- `src/world/textures.ts`: `makeStoneTexture(): THREE.CanvasTexture` per
  §4.23 (fill `#E6E2D8`, mortar `#C9C4B8`, 1-px lines every 32 px
  horizontally, vertical joints every 64 px staggered by 32 px on alternate
  courses, same wrap/repeat settings as `makeWindowTexture`). Keep the
  module browser-only like the window map.
- `src/main.ts`: choose `wallTex = cityInfo.facade === 'stone' ?
  makeStoneTexture() : windowTex` once, and pass it to every
  `makeBuildingsObject(...)` call (`grep -n makeBuildingsObject src/main.ts
  src/world/tiles.ts` — three sites; the tile-group path receives the
  texture through whatever `tiles.ts` already takes for the window map —
  extend that parameter, do not add a global).
- Tests: `tests/cities.test.ts` — `minas-tirith.facade === 'stone'`, every
  other city has no `facade`. `tests/textures.test.ts` (new): pure part —
  export a `stoneCourseAt(y: number): number` (course index for a pixel row,
  `floor(y / 32)`) and `stoneJointOffset(course): number` (`0` or `32` by
  parity), test both; the canvas builder itself is browser-only and not
  unit-tested.
- `e2e/minastirith.spec.ts`: extend the citadel test — sample the `#view`
  canvas (helper from `e2e/lowpoly.spec.ts`) after `aim(page, -Math.PI/2,
  0.3)` (facing west at the White Tower) and assert the fraction of
  near-black pixels (all channels < 40/255) is < 0.15 (window texture would
  give dark window squares; stone gives light masonry).
- `docs/minas-tirith.md`: a "Facade" paragraph.
