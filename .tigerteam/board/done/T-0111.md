fixes (colours/labels/heights where OSM stubs them), twelve spawn presets
with mechanically-tested vantages, and a default Circular Quay spawn that
frames the Opera House across the cove.

### Attempt-2 fix

**Root cause (PM diagnosis was correct).** Attempt 1's `harbourbridge`
preset (151.212236, −33.850223 → local (114.3, −1224.8)) sat on chain
vertex 11 of the joined 1503 m Cahill Walk crossing at arc-frac 0.73 —
30 m past the northern shore crossing on the Milsons Point foreshore,
not on the harbour deck. The old test's "≤ 3 m of a Cahill Walk piece"
matched vertex 11 (which IS a Cahill Walk vertex) without checking the
chain's arc-position or the resolved deck height, so the bad point sailed
through unit tests. The T-0059 corridor rule also passed because the
segment ENDS at vertex 11 (nothing to walk into within 40 m).

**New harbourbridge coordinate — derivation.** `chainBridgeRoads`
(src/world/terrain.ts:338) joins the four Cahill Walk `bridge=true`
pieces (segs 0, 1, 2, 5 in index.bridgeRoads) into ONE 25-vertex
polyline from (−392.0, −251.8) to (151.0, −1619.8), total arc length
1503.3 m. The `chainHumpApex` rule (T-0118) selects the longest same-name
chain for the Sydney Harbour Bridge hump (`deckApexASL = 49`,
apexY ≈ 45.1 m above datum). Arc-frac 0.500 (arc = 751.65 m) sits on
the 538 m long segment between chain vertices 10 (−135.6, −747.8) and
11 (114.3, −1224.8) at segment-t = 0.358 → local (−46.13, −918.58).
Unprojected via the Sydney origin (151.211, −33.8613): lon = 151.210501,
lat = −33.852993.

**Bearing 208° (NOT the PM's suggested 165–200°) — reason.** The v10→v11
segment orientation is 27.6° NNE; the reverse (south along the deck) is
207.6°. The Cahill Walk pedestrian corridor is 3 m halfWidth; if the
bearing points at Sydney Tower (SSE 184°) the forward walk drifts
sideways 0.397 m per metre and drops into the harbour water at k = 8 m,
FAILING the T-0059 corridor rule. Every bridge-deck preset in the game
faces along the deck (SF `ggb` 355° N, NYC `brooklynbridge` 316° NW —
both aligned with their deck axes). 208° faces SSW along the deck at
Wynyard / Barangaroo (western CBD skyline) and satisfies the corridor
rule with the 3.2 m halfWidth Cahill Walk exactly. The buildings-only
sightline test to Sydney Tower is bearing-independent (direct ray from
spawn to subject) so the Sydney-Tower postcard sightline still holds.

**Unit-test bindings (three facts, per ticket)**
1. **Chain identified by arc length** (`chainBridgeRoads` + longest
   Cahill Walk chain length in [1400, 1600] m) — not by name alone;
   distinguishes the harbour crossing from the 661 m Circular Quay
   walkway that shares the name.
2. **Distance ≤ 3 m of the chain AND arc-frac in [0.35, 0.65]** — the
   resolved spawn projects to the crossing chain's mid-span band.

   the way main.ts wires it (§4.9): the Sydney Harbour Bridge deck hump
