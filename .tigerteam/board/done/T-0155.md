## Goal
Add the reference's street furniture to the cyberpunk city: sodium street
lamps along the roads with faux-volumetric light cones and warm pools on the
wet ground, overhead cables strung across narrow streets, and a few real
point lights on the nearest lamps so they light the street. Contract:
docs/architecture.md §4.11 "`cyberpunk` v2 (wave 20b)" → "Street props" and
"Plumbing".

## Context
- Layer contract: `src/render/punk/layer.ts` (PM-owned). Stub
  `src/render/punk/props.ts` is registered and wired; replace its body, keep
  `export function createPropsLayer(): PunkLayer` with `id: 'props'` and
  `rainPassThrough: true`.
- Streaming: `CellStreamer` (`cells.ts`, PM-owned) buildRadius 450,
  disposeRadius 600, maxBuildsPerFrame 1; `build(cell)` returns a Group with
  (a) one merged opaque mesh (poles, arms, heads, cables), (b) one merged
  additive cone mesh, (c) one merged ground-pool mesh; share materials across
  cells. Roads come from `cell.roads` (`Road.pts`, `Road.cls`, `Road.bridge`).
- Pure builder `src/render/punk/propsmesh.ts` (NO `three/webgpu` import):
  `placeLamps(roads, heightAt) → Lamp[]` (`{ x, y, z, dirX, dirZ }`: base
  position and the unit direction the arm points, toward the road),
  `placeCables(roads, heightAt) → Cable[]` (catenary point lists), and
  `buildPropsMesh(lamps, cables) → { solid: MeshData, cones: MeshData, pools: MeshData }`
  using `MeshBuilder` from `src/world/mesh.ts`. Widths: import `ROAD_WIDTH`
  from `src/world/roads.ts`.
- Materials: heads `MeshStandardNodeMaterial` emissive (1.0, 0.55, 0.2) × 3;
  cones/pools `MeshBasicNodeMaterial` with `transparent`, `depthWrite: false`,
  `blending: THREE.AdditiveBlending`, opacity from a vertical / radial falloff
  node (`uv()` or `positionLocal` based), fog on.
- Point lights: ≤ 4 `THREE.PointLight` created once in `attach`, added to
  `root`, re-assigned every 0.5 s in `update` to the nearest lamps whose
  direction from the camera is within 60° of the view direction (use
  `ctx.camera.getWorldDirection`); remove them in `detach`. Stats:
  `lamps`, `cables`, `lights`, `triangles` + streamer stats.
- **Never open or read any `.png`/`.jpg`.**

## PM review — rework 1 (placement inside buildings)

Good work: the report, tests and wiring are all solid, and on the GPU the
lamps look right where they are visible (Westminster Bridge: sodium heads,
arm, cones). One real defect, which is the spec's fault, not yours:

**Lamps land inside buildings in narrow streets.** At `?city=london&at=bank`
the stats say 427 lamps / 12 cells are built, yet not one lamp is visible
along the street: the City's real streets are narrower than our class
widths, so `ROAD_WIDTH[cls]/2 + 0.8` puts the pole inside the facades.

Fix (all in scope):
1. `placeLamps(roads, heightAt, buildings?: readonly Building[])` — new
   optional 3rd argument. For each candidate lamp, if its base point lies
   inside any building footprint (point-in-polygon on `b.poly`) OR within
   0.6 m of a footprint edge, move it toward the centreline in 0.5 m steps
   down to a minimum offset of 1.5 m; if still blocked, skip that lamp.
   The arm keeps pointing at the road. Use a coarse spatial bucket (e.g.
   20 m grid of building indices) so this stays O(n) — `buildMs` per cell
   must stay ≤ 10 ms on london tile 0_0 cells.
2. `props.ts` passes `cell.buildings`.
3. Cables: keep their span, but clip each end to the first footprint edge
   it crosses from the centreline outward (so cables end on walls, not
   deep inside buildings); if a cable end is > 2 m inside a footprint after
   that, drop the cable.
4. New unit cases, by name:
   - "lamp is pushed out of a building that overhangs the class width": a
     primary road with a building whose wall is 4 m from the centreline →
     the lamp offset is ≤ 3.4 m and its base is outside the footprint;
   - "lamp is skipped when no free spot ≥ 1.5 m exists";
   - "no lamp base lies inside any footprint on london tile 0_0" (bucket
     with `bucketSources`, run per cell with `cell.buildings`), and report
     how many lamps survive (expect a drop; the 300–1200 tile range may
     need to become 200–1200 — if so, say so and use 200).
   - cable ends are within 2 m outside/on a footprint edge or at the
     span end when no building is hit.
5. Re-run everything in the acceptance list and paste the stats JSON at
   `bank` again — I'll re-check visibility on the GPU.
