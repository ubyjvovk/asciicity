## Goal
User review of the `quest` style: "wrong vibe — make it look more like a
drawing: flatter, thinner lines, without squares on the ground everywhere".
The revision is locked in `docs/architecture.md` §4.11, paragraph
"`quest` v2 (T-0130 …)" — read it first; it is the contract. The ramps,
palette and ramp selection stay exactly as they are.

## Context
- `src/render/styles/quest.ts`: (a) surfaces use six flat shades
  `QUEST_LEVELS = [2, 5, 9, 12, 16, 19]` via `level = min(5, floor(v·6))`,
  NO dither; (b) the outline test becomes one-sided (ink only on the nearer
  side: neighbour counts when `dN > dC`, or neighbour sky & centre not) and
  darkens by 2 shades, not 5; (c) vignette 0.15; (d) the sky is banded
  without dither per the three formulas. Keep the `qRamps` texture,
  `rampTint`, `daylight` uniforms and `update`/`dispose` as they are.
- Pure exports: `QUEST_LEVELS`, `shadeIndex(v)` (signature changes — no
  dither argument), `questSky(daylight, y01)` (banded), new
  `isNearSide(dC, dN, skyThr): boolean`. `bayer8` may stay (unused) or go
  with its test.
- `tests/styles/quest.test.ts` — update the `shadeIndex`/`questSky` cases
  to the new values and ADD, by name:
  1. `shadeIndex`: 0 → 2, 0.17 → 5, 0.5 → 12, 0.99 → 19, 1 → 19;
  2. `questSky(1, 0)` → `{8, 17}`, `questSky(1, 0.5)` → `{8, 14}` or `{8, 11}`
     per the formula (compute it), `questSky(1, 1)` → `{8, 8}`,
     `questSky(0, 1)` → `{9, 3}`;
  3. `isNearSide(10, 12, 1960)` → true; `isNearSide(12, 10, 1960)` → false;
     `isNearSide(10, 1965, 1960)` → true (neighbour sky); `isNearSide(1965,

- `e2e/quest.spec.ts`: thresholds per §4.11 — distinct colours ≥ 24,
  outlines 0.02–0.40; ADD assertion 7 (no full-width inked row below the
  horizon: for rows with `y > 0.55·height`, no row has ≥ 80 % of pixels at
  a shade index ≤ 4; quote the worst row). Keep the rest.
- `docs/styles/quest.md`: update the algorithm, the outline rule and the
  exports/uniforms tables.
