- Presets (names locked; coordinates are YOURS to derive from the data):
  `skytree`, `tokyotower`, `tokyostation`, `ginza`, `akihabara`,
  `imperialpalace`, `sumida` (a riverside vantage that faces the Skytree

  `index.landmarks`) near the target, choose bearing to FACE the landmark,
  and prove every preset walkable with a unit test that builds a
  `CollisionGrid` from the preset's 3×3 tiles (fs-read in node) and
  asserts `blocked(preset) === false` and ground sanity. Tower presets are
  street vantages LOOKING AT the tower, not points inside its footprint.

  find in `index.landmarks` (T-0097's report lists them) — add further
  entries only if the data shows a landmark rendering absurdly (flag in
  report).
- Names in HUD/tags will be `name:en` where OSM has it, else Japanese —
  both render; never transliterate (AGENTS.md).
