## Goal
The user: "a gigantic female hologram (like 50 m tall), like in Blade Runner
2049 … any reasonable anime-ish model is fine; placement — very central and
prominent in each city". The PM sourced and committed the model:
`public/models/holo/mia.glb` (44.5 k triangles, 16 skinned meshes on one
61-joint skin, one Mixamo dance clip "mixamo.com", 4.10 s loop; 9 materials,
512² WebP textures; bind-pose bbox 3.15 × 3.62 × 0.66 in model units, y up).
Credits already in `public/models/holo/CREDITS.md` + README.

## Design (LOCKED — flag, don't redesign)
Read first: architecture.md §4.11 (layer API) and **§4.11 "Stutter rules"
(mandatory)**, `punk/layer.ts`, and `punk/vehicles.ts` (GLTFLoader usage,
lazy load, warm-up pattern — copy it).

New layer id `'holo'`, registered in `layers.ts` after `props` (exactly one
import + one array entry; another ticket adds `ads` after `neon` — keep your
diff to your own line). `rainPassThrough: true`.

**Anchors (pure, `hologramplace.ts`, no three import):**
`HOLO_ANCHORS: Record<cityId, { preset: string; aheadM: number }>` =
london → `bank`, kyiv → `maidan`, tokyo → `shibuya`, sydney →
`circularquay`, sf → `unionsquare`, nyc → `timessquare` (all `aheadM`
40); no hologram for synthetic / minas-tirith. Resolve the preset with the
existing spawn resolver (`src/data/spawn.ts`, `resolveSpawn` / `SPAWN_PRESETS`;
read-only), step `aheadM` metres along the preset bearing, then pick the
nearest point (search rings every 5 m up to 80 m) whose 14 m radius disc
contains no building footprint vertex / edge and whose centre is not inside
any footprint — pure function `holoSpot(start, bearing, buildings) → {x, z}
| null`. The hologram FACES the preset position (the default spawn view sees
her front). Tiled cities: compute once the tiles around the anchor are
resident (ctx.sources); keep it fixed afterwards.

**Model & animation:** lazy GLTFLoader load on first attach (cached across
activations). `AnimationMixer` playing the clip in a loop at 0.85× speed;
advance it in `update` with `dtS`. Scale uniformly so the posed height =
50 m; feet on `groundAt(x, z)`. Very slow sway: yaw oscillates ±20° around
the facing direction with a 40 s period. Draws: merge where cheap, else ≤ 16
draws; one shared hologram material (clones only if textures differ).

**Hologram look (TSL, additive):** `transparent`, `depthWrite false`,
`AdditiveBlending`, `side DoubleSide`, `fog false` (it must glow through
the haze), emissive into the bloom path like neon faces.
- colour = texture luminance (keep features readable: face, hair, clothes)
  × a tint gradient over model height (cyan 0x3ef2ff at the feet → magenta
  0xff3ad8 at the head), plus a fresnel rim (view-dependent, bright edges);
- horizontal scanlines in world y (period 0.35 m, scrolling up 0.6 m/s)
  modulating 0.55–1.0; fine noise shimmer;
- opacity/brightness flicker: base 0.75, a ±0.08 1–3 Hz wobble, and every
  6–14 s (hash of time) a 0.15 s glitch: horizontal band displacement of
  vertices (bands 1.5 m tall shifted ±0.6 m in x) + brightness drop;
- base: a projector disc on the ground (radius 9 m, emissive cyan ring +
  faint radial glow) and a faint light column (additive cylinder, radius
  7 m, height 55 m, opacity ≤ 0.06, fading upward). No real lights.
- Close up (< 30 m) it must not white out: clamp so bloom keeps the
  silhouette/features legible; far away (0.5–2 km) it must stay visible
  above the rooftops (`fog false` + moderate emissive).

**Stutter rules:** the model loads lazily — while loading nothing shows;
before the first reveal draw it once warmed (scale ≈ 0, or a zero-matrix)
through the real pipeline, then reveal next frame. No lights, no
visibility toggling of lights. Skinning cost: one skeleton, fine.

### For the reviewer — decisions / flags
1. **City origin.** `PunkLayerContext` has no origin, and `resolveSpawn` needs one. `HOLO_ORIGINS` mirrors every `index.json` `origin`, and the unit test `HOLO_ORIGINS match every index.json origin` fails on drift. The alternative was re-fetching and parsing the index (up to 2.7 MB) in the layer, which would cause a main-thread hitch. A cleaner fix is to add `origin` to `PunkLayerContext`, but that means editing PM-owned `layer.ts` / `main.ts`.
2. **`holoSpot(start, bearing, buildings, aheadM = 0)`.** `start` is the stepped start. `bearing` orders the ring samples and does the optional step. `holoPlacement` wires preset → step → spot → yaw.
3. **Tiled wait.** She is placed once the start's own tile is resident and either all tiles within 400 m of the start are resident or the resident set has been unchanged for 2 s. Tiles beyond the dataset, or outside the player's 2 km streaming square, never arrive. Example: from London's Big Ben spawn, tiles `0_−1` and `0_0` are out of range. The ticket says "keep it fixed afterwards"; I added one exception. If a tile that was missing later arrives and its footprints cut the 14 m disc, she is re-placed over all anchor tiles (`holo.moves`). Otherwise she never moves. For London the partial placement equals the full-data spot.
4. **Post fog.** `fog false` only skips the scene height fog. The PM-owned post atmosphere (`atmosphere.ts`) still hazes her using the depth behind her, because she does not write depth: at most `fogEnabled·fogAmount` = 0.28 with the default looks, plus bloom suppression. She stays visible. If she needs to be fully fog-immune, that is a pipeline change.
5. **Bloom routing.** A material `mrtNode` overrides the `emissive` target, scaled ×0.15 close to ×0.6 far so she does not white out under 30 m. It writes alpha 0 into the `normal` and `metalrough` targets so SSR under her is unchanged. The per-target blend modes come from the pass MRT (`renderer.getMRT()`), so the override is safe.
6. **Draws.** 16 skinned meshes (not merged) + disc + column = 18. Five hologram materials: hair, suit/jewellery atlas, face, body, and the untextured face-screen part.
7. **Placement distances.** London's spot is only ~21 m from the `bank` preset, and SF's and NYC's sit exactly on the stepped start. Close-up legibility at the London spawn preset is worth a GPU look.
8. Out-of-scope observation: `MRTNode.merge` in three 0.185 assigns `mrtTarget.blendings`, a typo for `blendModes`. It is harmless here because pipelines read the pass MRT's blend modes.
