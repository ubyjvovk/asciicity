## PM rework note 1 (2026-09-18) — spec fix, not your fault

Your shader matches §4.11 v1 exactly, and that formula was wrong: on
every coloured wall the mix factor clamps to 1, so the frames are flat
cel fills with no granulation and a plain grey ground. §4.11 "watercolor"
is amended on `main` (read it with `git show main:docs/architecture.md |
grep -n "watercolor" `). The v2 formula, in full:

    pig  = mix(vec3(1.0), tint, 0.8 · satF)
    dens = 0.25 + 0.55 · pow(tone2, 0.6)
    gran = 0.70 + 0.60 · blotch(p)
    mixF = clamp(dens · gran · (0.35 + 0.65 · satF), 0, 0.9)
    col  = mix(PAPER_W, pig, mixF)
    grey: col = mix(col, GREY_WASH, (1 − satF) · tone2 · 0.5 · gran)          // GREY_WASH = (0.35, 0.36, 0.42)
    bloom: col = mix(col, PAPER_W, 0.25 · smoothstep(0.60, 0.90, vnoise(p / 40)))
    pooled edge: col · 0.55 on s.outline (unchanged)
    sky day: as before · gran, then col = mix(col, (0.97, 0.98, 1.0), 0.6 · smoothstep(0.55, 0.80, vnoise(p / 30)))
    sky night: unchanged

Update the pure mirrors (`washDensity` → 0.25 + 0.55·tone^0.6,
`washStrength`/`watercolorWash` → the new mixF and grey term; add
`bloom(x, y)` = the smoothstep term) and their unit cases (the "paper at
tone 0" case now expects `mixF ≤ 0.25·1.3·1.0 = 0.325` at most, and
the tone-0 grey wash to be 0), and `docs/styles/watercolor.md`. Then the
acceptance commands, `bash scripts/check.sh` end to end, commit, hand
off. Do not open any `.png`.
