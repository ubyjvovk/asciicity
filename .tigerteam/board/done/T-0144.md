## Goal
User review: "in pastel there's a static noise mask on top of everything —
look into the sky and it never changes". The paper grain and the blotch
noise of the four sketch styles are screen-space. Move them into the
shared chunk as world-anchored (surfaces) / dome-anchored (sky) noise with
a blended power-of-two LOD, exactly as locked in `docs/architecture.md`
§4.11, subsection "Sketch family — anchored tooth (wave 18b, T-0144)" —
read it first; it is the contract. Colour formulas do not change; only
the noise sources.

## Context
- `strokes.ts` gains GLSL + TS mirrors: `anchoredNoise(x, y, mx, my,
  cells, key)`, `vnoiseA(...)`, `toothOf(u, along, mu, ma)`,
  `blotchA(u, along, mu, ma)`, `bloomA(u, along, mu, ma)` (the GLSL takes
  a `Surf` where the doc says `(s)`; sky branch uses `s.ps` with
  `mx = my = 1`). Read `docs/styles/strokes.md` for the chunk and the
  `Surf` fields (`u`, `along`, `mu`, `ma`, `ps` are all there).
- Each style: replace its local `g` / `blotch` / `vnoise` / `bloom` with
  the chunk calls; delete the local definitions (watercolor and pastel
  carry TS mirrors and tests for `vnoise`/`blotch` — move those tests to
  `tests/styles/strokes.test.ts` in their new form, delete the old).
- Unit cases in `tests/styles/strokes.test.ts`, by name: (1)
  `anchoredNoise` no-pop across a power-of-two boundary (200 seeded
  points, `j ∈ {−4, −2}`, |Δ| < 0.05); (2) mean 0.5 ± 0.1 at three
  scales; (3) `vnoiseA` continuity (|Δ| < 0.02 per 1e−3 cell); (4)
  `toothOf` in [0.8, 1.2]; (5) `blotchA`, `bloomA` in [0, 1].
- Docs: `strokes.md` documents the five helpers; the four style docs
  replace their grain/blotch paragraphs with a pointer.
- Never open any `.png`; `ls -la` only. The PM does the visual review
  (a sky pan and a walk in `pastel`).
