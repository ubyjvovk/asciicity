## Goal
Add water to `city.json` so the Thames (south edge of the bbox) and the docks
render as flat blue areas instead of empty ground. The data contract already
has `water?: Vec2[][]` (`src/data/types.ts`, `docs/data-format.md`).

## Context
- Overpass query additions (append to the union in `scripts/fetch-osm.mjs`):
  `way["natural"="water"](bbox); way["waterway"="riverbank"](bbox);
  relation["natural"="water"](bbox); relation["waterway"="riverbank"](bbox);`
  With `out geom;` relation members carry full geometry — the River Thames
  relation extends far beyond the bbox, hence clipping below.
- Converter (`scripts/osm-convert.mjs`), new pure exports:
  - `assembleRings(ways: Array<{ id: number; geometry: Array<{ lon: number; lat: number }> }>): Array<Array<{ lon: number; lat: number }>>`
    — closed ways become rings directly; open ways are chained greedily by
    matching endpoints (equal lon/lat within 1e-7) until closed; unclosed
    chains are dropped (count them in the summary line as `dropped N open water chains`).
  - `clipRingToBox(ring: Vec2[], box: { minX: number; minZ: number; maxX: number; maxZ: number }): Vec2[]`
    — Sutherland–Hodgman against the four edges; may return < 3 points.
  - Water rings = `outer` members of water relations + standalone water ways,
    assembled, projected to local metres via `toRing`-style rounding, clipped
    to the bbox **expanded by 300 m** in local metres, cleaned (same
    duplicate rules as buildings), dropped if < 3 points or |area| < 25 m².
    Emit as `water: Vec2[][]`; omit the key when empty.
- `src/data/validate.ts`: accept optional `water` (array of rings with the
  `poly` rules; error paths `water[i]`). `tests/data.test.ts`: add accept /
  reject (2-point ring) cases.
- Fixture: add one closed `natural=water` way, one water relation whose
  outer is split across two open ways that chain into a closed ring, and one
  open way that cannot close (dropped). Regenerate `public/data/city.json`
  (Overpass; counts of buildings/roads/places must stay within ±5 % of
  `3118 / 10307 / 43`). The Thames must be present: assert in the dataset
  test that some water ring has a point with `z > 600` (the river is ~700 m
  south of Bank) and that every ring passes `validateCity`.
- Update `docs/fetch-osm.md` (water section, summary line).
