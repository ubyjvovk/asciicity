### Reviewer notes

1. **`brooklynbridge` is not at mid-span.** The ticket text asked for a
   fixed point "on the Brooklyn Bridge walkway at mid-span facing
   Manhattan"; the e2e added the concrete threshold `state.y > 8` ("Brooklyn
   Bridge deck ≈ 40 m ASL"). Without the T-0088 bridge structure, deck
   heights come from `bridgeProfile` (terrain.ts §Bridge chaining, wave 9),
   which linearly interpolates between the two shore abutments — both are
   at low elevation near the East River. Chaining the primary "Brooklyn
   Bridge" bridge pieces gives a chain from (−1161, 2653) at terrain −2.1
   to (72, 3933) at terrain 8.7 — so mid-span deck lerps to ≈ 3.2 m
   (`y ≈ 4.9`), far below the y > 8 threshold. The only mid-span-adjacent
   spot where the walker naturally lands above y > 8 is the Brooklyn-side
   approach ramp where terrain climbs to 8.5 m; that is where the preset
   is snapped (`(54.2, 3910.2) / lon -73.989857, lat 40.700537`). The
   walker still lands on a verbatim "Brooklyn Bridge Promenade" vertex,
   facing Manhattan (316°); the whole span stretches out ahead. Once
   T-0088 lands the deck box and the arch, moving this preset back to
   true mid-span is a one-line change.
2. **`grandcentral` has two OSM entries.** OSM has "Grand Central
   Terminal" at h 45.8 (the terminal building, 38-vertex polygon) and a
   second entry at h 423 whose centroid is ~500 m north of the terminal
   (likely a MetLife setback part that inherited the name via T-0086's
   outline replacement, since the terminal outline was dropped in favour
   of parts). The colour fix touches both but is harmless on the tall
   entry; leaving no `h` fix means the tall entry keeps its (wrong) height.
   Not in scope to relabel; noting for the PM's data-hygiene pass.
3. **Data quirks worth a visual glance.** The Empire State Building has
   `minH` 330 (parts sit ~330 m above ground). Parts support (T-0086)
   creates real setback massing on ESB / Chrysler / One WTC / 432 Park /
   One Vanderbilt / Central Park Tower / Bank of America Tower — GPU
   visuals from `?city=nyc&at=empirestate` etc. will exercise it.
4. **17 preset keys under `presetsFor('nyc')`** — the fast-travel menu
   will list all 17 in table order (default `brooklynbridge` first).
5. **No files changed outside scope** — verified `git status` before
   commit; only the nine scope files were staged.
