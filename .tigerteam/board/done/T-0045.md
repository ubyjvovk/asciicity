## Goal
Kyiv is playable: `?city=kyiv` loads `data/kyiv.json`, the heightfield is
drawn, the player/camera/sky/buses walk on `groundAt` (terrain + bridge
decks), boats float on the river level, the HUD shows `ALT`, and named
Kyiv spawn presets exist. London (`?city=london`, and the default when no
`?city=` is given — the picker is T-0046) is byte-for-byte unchanged in
behaviour.
