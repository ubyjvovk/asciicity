### For the reviewer
- **Budget wording:** architecture §4.2 says tiers "add ≤ 2×" the envelope triangles, but the ticket says "tiers ≤ 2×". I tested the stricter reading (tiered total ≤ 2 × envelope). The synthetic set is every london `0_0` envelope cut into 3 x-slab tiers: ratio 1.36. The hand-made 3-tier building on a single rectangle comes out at 34 triangles against 10 (×3.4). Any simple box split into tiers exceeds ×2, so the budget is only meaningful over a realistic set.
- `exteriorWalls` for a tier-less building with `roof` returns the LOD1 span (`top + h`), not the wall-top under the roof. This matches what detail/neon used before, which is what keeps them byte-identical. The existing behaviour of detail ledges/pipes reaching into pitched roofs is unchanged, and out of scope here.
- For neon, the "sign must fit inside the segment" check runs only on tiered buildings. Applying it to envelope walls would drop some existing signs on 8–11 m buildings and break byte-identity.
- Detail AC floors and the 40 m AC cap are still measured from the building's wall base, not the segment base. So a tower wall that starts at 20 m only gets ACs between 20 and 40 m.
- In the mesh, `buildBuildingsMesh` still computes the (ignored) `roof` frame for tiered buildings before dropping it. This is harmless because data-format forbids having both `roof` and `tiers`.
