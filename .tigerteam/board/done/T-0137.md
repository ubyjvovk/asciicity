## Goal
User review of `scribble` v2: "looks like it has a fixed grid overlay of
irregular lines that looks out of place". The strokes are drawn in screen
space, so the pattern stays put while the world slides under it. v3 anchors
every stroke to the world and keeps the on-screen spacing constant with a
nested power-of-two level of detail; the sky tangle sticks to the sky dome.
Every formula is locked in `docs/architecture.md` §4.11, subsection
"`scribble` v3 (T-0137)" — read it first; it is the contract. Colour,
outline, tone, thresholds, half-widths, lifts and the ground/wall class
rule stay exactly as v2.

### Reviewer notes
- Nested LOD takes max coverage over the nearest line and `i0 ± 2` of each family. Spec writes `i = floor(u/S+0.5)` (nearest only); with stroke width + wobble the nearest-only form pops at a LOD boundary (odd line fades, even neighbour never evaluated). The extra samples are what makes the mechanical no-pop criterion hold.
- Ground `u` is metres of depth. The scene target is 1 sample/cell, so `linearDepth(vUv)` equals cell-centre `dC` for every pixel in the cell. The shader reconstructs a per-pixel depth from the one-cell taps (`dC + ½(dR−dL)·ox + ½(dU−dD)·oy`, gradient zeroed on a 35 % jump) so constant-depth lines are continuous at canvas resolution. Without that, whole 3 px cells ink on/off and the e2e “ground strokes are horizontal” run-count fails (813 horiz vs 837 vert; with the reconstruction 813 vs 456).

## PM rework note 1 (2026-09-18) — spec fix, not your fault

Your anchoring works: on the host GPU the strokes move with the world when
the camera walks 1 m and the wall strokes converge when it looks up. Two
defects come from the PM's spec, now amended in `docs/architecture.md`
§4.11 "scribble v3" on `main` (read it with
`git show main:docs/architecture.md | grep -n "scribble. v3" ` — your
worktree branch predates the amendment). Both of your reviewer notes
(±2 neighbour lines, per-pixel ground depth `dPix`) are adopted into the
spec — keep them.

**1. Stroke scale must come from the SCREEN gradient of u, not from
`m = dC·2·tanHalfFov/sceneSize.y`.** `m` is only right for a surface
facing the camera. On the ground and on an oblique facade `u` changes
many metres per screen cell, so the lines fall far below a pixel and the
surface renders as static (that is what the frames show: the road and
the long purple facade are speckle, the frontal Tokyo facades are
perfect). Per cell, from the five taps you already have:

    W_N = (viewToWorld · vec4(viewPos(uv_N, d_N), 1)).xyz   for N ∈ {L, R, U, D}
    u_N, along_N = strokeCoords(W_N, nW, d_N, up)            // ground: u_N = d_N
    wall:   mu = max(|u_R − u_L| / 2, 1e−4);   ma = max(|along_U − along_D| / 2, 1e−4)
    ground: mu = max(|u_U − u_D| / 2, 1e−4);   ma = max(|along_R − along_L| / 2, 1e−4)

`mu` (metres of u per cell across the strokes) replaces `m` in `lod`,
`hw`, the smoothstep width and the wobble amplitude; `ma` (metres of
along per cell) replaces `m` in the wobble frequency (`along / ma`) and
the lift segment (`along / (24·ma)`). New pure export
`strokeScale(uN: { L, R, U, D }, alongN: { L, R, U, D }, up): { mu, ma }`;
`nestedStrokeInk(u, along, tone, mu, ma)` and `lodOf(mu)` take the new
scale. Unit tests: keep cases 3–6 with `mu = ma = m`; add
`strokeScale` — wall taps `u = {L: 1, R: 3}`, `along = {U: 7, D: 5}` →
`{ mu: 1, ma: 1 }`; ground taps `u = {U: 25, D: 20}`, `along = {L: 0, R: 4}`
→ `{ mu: 2.5, ma: 2 }`; and degenerate `u = {L: 5, R: 5}` → `mu = 1e−4`.

**2. Sky mapping: stereographic from the nadir instead of atan/asin.**
Looking up, the atan/asin map draws a starburst pole at the zenith.
Replace with

    ps = K · vec2(dirW.x, dirW.z) / (1 + dirW.y)

(the only pole is straight down, never sky; no seam). `skyCoords` test
values become: `[0, 0, −1]` → `[0, −100]`, `[0, 1, 0]` → `[0, 0]`,
`[1, 0, 0]` → `[100, 0]` (K = 100).

Then rerun the three acceptance commands, quote the e2e measurements,
update `docs/styles/scribble.md` for both changes, commit, hand off.
Do not open any `.png`.
