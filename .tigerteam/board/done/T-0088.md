## Goal
Manhattan's East River bridges start at street level on both banks, so the
straight abutment-to-abutment lerp puts the Brooklyn Bridge deck ~3 m over
the river (T-0087 report, note 1); the real deck is 41 m ASL at mid-span.
Add deck humps driven by the bridge spec, generalise the spec to
one-walkway bridges, build the three NYC structures, and move
`brooklynbridge` to true mid-span. Contracts: `docs/architecture.md` §4.9
"Deck humps (wave 10)" and §4.16 (`sidewalks: [string] | [string,
string]`, `deckWidth`, `deckApexASL`, `deckRoads`, `deckHumps`, the
one-walkway frame rule) — read both in full first.

### Reviewer notes
- **`deckWidth` vs measured sep.** Two-walkway GGB still uses the OSM median (no `deckWidth`). NYC specs that set `deckWidth` use it as `sep`. Williamsburg's OSM pair sit ~13 m apart on a 36 m pylon; using the median would make a 13 m deck. Architecture §4.16 only names `deckWidth` for one-walkway; this is the smallest way to honour the listed 36 m / 37 m widths. Brooklyn is true one-walkway (`Promenade` only).
- **Cable sag.** `sag = (topS+topN)/2 − deckY(mid) − 3` so a hump still has 3 m mid-span clearance. Flat decks (existing GGB unit tests) keep `towerTopAboveDeck − 3`.
- **Hump densify.** `bridgeProfile` still evaluates at vertices; `BridgeDecks` / ribbons densify named humped roads to ≤ 50 m so OSM's 400 m+ main-span chords do not flatten the parabola (Manhattan pedestrian path was 1.45 m low at mid-span before this).
- **Struts.** Levels at 70/110/150 m sit above Brooklyn's 43 m towers; those boxes are skipped. GGB 160 m is unchanged.
- **Brooklyn wrap.** Promenade wraps the pylons (nearest-point ~3.8 m); the tower test averages all stations at that along (GGB mid-line style) and passes at ~0.2 m from the through-line.
- Out of scope, not touched: collision on the structures, gothic tower shapes, converter/re-fetch, ships. GPU look of the three NYC spans is for the PM visual pass.
