## Goal
Press `F` to take off: fly where you look (mouse pitch steers), `Space`/`C`
for straight up/down, `Shift` for 90 m/s, no collision while airborne,
ground-clamped, 1 500 m ceiling; fog thins with altitude so the whole city
is visible from above; `F` again lands (constant-speed fall). Full contract:
`docs/architecture.md` §4.7 (updated 2026-08-26) and its "Frame loop
consequences" paragraph — implement every signature and rule exactly.
