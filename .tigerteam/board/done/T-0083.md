## Goal
T-0082 chained fragmented bridge roads for the walkable deck (`BridgeDecks`),
but `buildRoadsMesh` (`src/world/roads.ts` ≈ line 52) still computes
`bridgeProfile(pts, heightAt)` per road piece, so on the Golden Gate Bridge
the motorway ribbon and the East Sidewalk ribbon lerp between their own
underwater joints and vanish below the deck for two-thirds of the span (PM
GPU frame from mid-span: solid orange deck, no road). Drape ribbons over the
same chained polylines. Contract: `docs/architecture.md` §4.4/§4.9 road
bullet ("over `chainBridgeRoads(roads)` (wave 9, T-0083)").

## Context
- `src/world/roads.ts`: `import { bridgeProfile, chainBridgeRoads } from
  './terrain'` and iterate `for (const road of chainBridgeRoads(roads))` in
  `buildRoadsMesh`. Nothing else changes (widths, colours, the +0.15 lift,
  `makeRoadsObject`). `chainBridgeRoads` is pure and leaves non-bridge and
  unnamed roads untouched, so London/Kyiv meshes are byte-identical.
- `tests/roads.test.ts` — keep every existing case, add:
  - `'a same-name bridge split into three pieces with underwater joints is draped as one deck'`
    (synthetic `HeightFn`: abutments 30 and 46, water −24 between; assert
    the ribbon vertices of the middle piece sit at the chained lerp + 0.15,
    not at −24 + 0.15)
  - `'sf.json: every Golden Gate Bridge ribbon vertex is above 25 m'`
    (load `public/data/sf.json` like `tests/landmarks.test.ts`, build
    `Terrain` + `BridgeDecks` + `makeGroundAt` exactly as `src/main.ts` does,
    call `buildRoadsMesh` on the roads named `Golden Gate Bridge` and both
    `… Sidewalk` roads only, and assert every position y ≥ 25)
