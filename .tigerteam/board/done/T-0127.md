## Goal
Fill the seven circles: the tangential house rows on tiers 1–6, the
Citadel (White Tower of Ecthelion, Tower Hall, Merethrond, The King's
House), Houses of Healing, Rath Dínen's tombs, The Old Guesthouse, the
White Tree and the garden trees — every number per `docs/architecture.md`
§4.23 "Houses" and "Citadel and landmarks". Then run the generator and
commit `public/data/minas-tirith/` (the tiled dataset).

## Context
- New module `scripts/minas-tirith-buildings.mjs` exporting
  `buildHouses(tiers, gateAz, rand)`, `buildLandmarks()`, `buildTrees()`;
  `scripts/gen-minas-tirith.mjs` (T-0126) imports them in `buildCity` and
  appends them to `buildings`/`trees`. Reuse T-0126's `TIERS`, `GATE_AZ`,
  `mulberry32`, and its lane geometry to honour the "skip footprints that
  cross a radial lane (±6 m)" rule.
- Houses: ids `10000 + k·1000 + n`; landmark ids `20000 + n`; every
  building carries `color` per §4.23; landmarks carry `name` (and `shape`
  where listed) so `applyLandmarks`/HUD/tags treat them as landmarks.
- Unit cases (`tests/minastirith-buildings.test.ts`), by name:
  1. every house lies on its tier's plateau: `terrainHeight` at the
     footprint centroid equals `H_k` (±0.5) and never inside the prow sector
     or a gate approach;
  2. no two houses overlap (AABB test on footprints);
  3. no house footprint crosses a radial lane centre-line (±6 m);
  4. `buildLandmarks` yields the 12 named buildings of §4.23 with the given
     sizes/heights/shapes (check `White Tower of Ecthelion` h 90 shape
     tower, 6 Rath Dínen domes, `House of the Stewards` 12 m);
  5. `buildTrees` → 7 entries, `[0, 0, 8, 3]` first;
  6. `buildCity(1)` still validates and is deterministic.
- Run `npm run gen-data:minas-tirith`, commit `public/data/minas-tirith/`
  (index.json + tiles). Quote `stat -c %s public/data/minas-tirith/index.json`
  in the report (T-0128 needs it for `sizeBytes`). Inspect with `node -e`
  / `jq` only — never `cat` the tiles.
- Update `docs/minas-tirith.md` with the buildings section and the
  landmark list.
