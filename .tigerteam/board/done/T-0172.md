## Goal
The user, walking Sydney in `cyberpunk`: the distant highrises "are really
begging for huge neon ads which dominate cyberpunk cityscapes" (think Blade
Runner / Ghost in the Shell skylines). The PM already made highrise tops
readable (commit 31da12c: height-attenuated fog + light-pollution sky); the
towers are now dark silhouettes. Dress them: giant animated ad screens on
tall buildings, visible from across the city, plus red aviation beacons and
roofline LED strips. Street-level neon (neon.ts) is a different layer — do
not touch it.

## Design (LOCKED — flag, don't redesign)
Read first: architecture.md §4.11 "cyberpunk v2" (layer API, CellStreamer),
**§4.11 "Stutter rules" (mandatory)**, `punk/layer.ts`, `punk/cells.ts`,
and `punk/neon.ts` + `neonatlas.ts` as the pattern to copy (atlas canvas,
TSL materials, CellStreamer usage).

New layer id `'ads'`, registered in `layers.ts` after `neon` (exactly one
import + one array entry). `rainPassThrough: true`.

**Placement (pure, `megaadsplace.ts`, no three import):**
`placeMegaAds(buildings, roads, groundAt, cityId, seed?) → MegaAd[]`, stable
per building id (hash, no Math.random).
- Tall building = envelope `h ≥ 45` m (for PLATEAU `tiers`, use the max tier
  top). Skip `minH ≥ 2.5` parts.
- **Facade screens**: h ≥ 80 → always 1 screen; h ≥ 150 → 2 screens on
  roughly opposite faces; 45 ≤ h < 80 → p = 0.55. A screen sits on the
  footprint edge whose outward normal faces the nearest road (fallback:
  longest edge), centred on the edge, offset 0.8 m outward, top edge 4–10 %
  of h below the roof, height = clamp(0.3·h, 14, 70) m, width =
  min(edge length − 2 m, height × aspect) with aspect 0.45–0.7 (portrait)
  or, for edges ≥ 40 m, a landscape 1.6–2.2. Skip edges shorter than 10 m.
- **Roof billboards**: 25 ≤ h < 45, p = 0.12 (Tokyo/NYC 0.25): a
  landscape board 12–20 m wide, 5–8 m tall on 3 m legs at the roof, facing
  the nearest road; frame + legs as dark metal geometry.
- **Aviation beacons**: h ≥ 60 → 1–4 red beacons at roof corners (+ one at
  the top of any tier tower).
- **Roofline strips**: h ≥ 60, p = 0.5 → an emissive strip 0.35 m tall
  along the roof perimeter, colour from the cyberpunk palette.
- Caps per 250 m cell: ≤ 24 screens + billboards, ≤ 64 beacons.
- Density check (test): Sydney CBD tile(s) and Tokyo Shinjuku yield ≥ 30
  screens in range of a 1.5 km radius; London City ≥ 12; Kyiv ≥ 4.

**Content (`megaadsatlas.ts`, browser; canvas atlas like neonatlas):** one
2048² canvas, 16 portrait slots (512×1024) + 8 landscape (1024×512 → pack as
you see fit), drawn ONCE at attach. Invented lore brands only (NO real
brands/logos): a word list you write, e.g. NEUROCOLA, SYNTHLOVE, YUKI-TEC,
ORBITAL AIR, DREAMCHIP, OMNIHEALTH, KAGE MOTORS, plus katakana/kanji
phrases (ネオン, 未来, 夢, 愛, 電脳), and per-city flavour (Cyrillic for
Kyiv: НЕОН, МРІЯ). Designs: big wordmark + geometric logo (circle/
triangle/eye/wave), gradient background, a slogan line; high-saturation
palette (magenta, cyan, amber, acid green, hot red). A few "face" slots may
use a stylised geometric silhouette — nothing photographic.

**Look (`megaads.ts`, TSL):** emissive screen material sampling the atlas;
per-screen slot index + phase in vertex attributes. Every 7–12 s (per-screen
phase) switch slot with a 0.3 s glitch transition (horizontal band offsets
+ RGB split); slow vertical marquee on portrait slots; fine scanlines +
RGB subpixel grid visible within ~60 m; brightness ×(0.9–1.1) pulse.
Emissive strong enough to bloom (compare neon faces) but screens must stay
READABLE (not white blobs) at 50–300 m. Beacons: small emissive quads (or
tiny boxes) blinking 1 Hz red, phase per building. Strips: emissive, no
blink. `fog = true` (the height fog keeps high screens clear).

**Streaming & budgets:** its own CellStreamer with buildRadius 1600 m,
disposeRadius 1900 m, maxBuildsPerFrame 1 — screens must be visible from
across a city. Per cell ≤ 3 draw calls (screens, frames+legs, beacons+strips
merged by material), merged geometry per cell, no per-sign Object3D. Whole
layer ≤ 150 k triangles at `sydney&at=harbourbridge` and
`tokyo&at=shinjuku`; any cell build ≤ 8 ms in node (placement + mesh).
**No lights** (emissive only). Obey the Stutter rules: every material
created at attach and warmed (one zero-matrix / zero-area draw) before the
first real cell appears; never toggle light visibility (there are none).
