## Goal
A Playwright spec that proves the `scribble` style (T-0134,
`docs/architecture.md` §4.11 "`scribble` (wave 17)") really draws
coloured ink strokes on white paper, orients them by surface, and tangles
the sky — by measuring pixels like `e2e/quest.spec.ts`.

## Context
- New file `e2e/scribble.spec.ts`. Copy the boot helpers (`waitReady`,
  `doubleRaf`, the offscreen-canvas sampler, the `aim` helper) from
  `e2e/quest.spec.ts`; never edit other specs.
- Import `PAPER`, `INK`, `SKY_INK` from `../src/render/styles/scribble`.
  "Paper" = within 10/255 per channel of `PAPER`; "ink" = max channel
  < 0.35; "coloured ink" = max channel < 0.7 and `max − min` ≥ 0.15.
- URL A: `/?synthetic=1&render=scribble&cell=3x3&time=12:00`; after ready +
  two double-rAF waits, sample the un-aimed frame, then `aim(page, 0,
  0.9)` (north, tilted up) and sample again for the sky assertions.
  URL B: same with `time=23:00`, aimed.
- Assertions, by name:
  1. paper: in the un-aimed noon frame, paper pixels are between 0.20
     and 0.85 of all pixels (it is a sketch on white, not solid ink);
  2. ink: ink pixels are between 0.05 and 0.60;
  3. coloured ink: ≥ 0.01 of pixels are coloured ink and at least 3
     distinct hue bins (12 × 30° bins on the pixel's hue) each hold ≥ 0.1 %
     of pixels — the buildings carry their own ink colours;
  4. wall strokes are vertical: over the middle third of the frame's
     width and the rows between 0.10 and 0.45 of the height measured from
     the top (buildings above the horizon), the count of
     vertical runs of ≥ 6 consecutive ink pixels exceeds the count of
     horizontal runs of ≥ 6 consecutive ink pixels by a factor ≥ 1.5
     (walk columns for vertical runs, rows for horizontal; count a run once
     when it ends);
  5. ground strokes are horizontal: over the bottom 20 % of rows, the
     horizontal-run count exceeds the vertical-run count by a factor ≥ 1.5;
  6. sky (URL A, aimed): the top 30 % of rows are ≥ 0.55 paper and hold
     ≥ 0.01 ink (the tangle); (URL B, aimed) the ink fraction of the top
     30 % is greater than URL A's (night tangle is denser);
  7. no full-width inked row: no row below the horizon (bottom 45 % of
     the un-aimed noon frame) has ≥ 0.98 ink pixels (same rule as lowpoly
     v2 / quest v2).
- If a threshold fails by a small margin, report the measured numbers and
  adjust only within: paper 0.15–0.90, ink 0.03–0.70, coloured ≥ 0.005,
  hue bins ≥ 2, run-count factors ≥ 1.2, sky paper ≥ 0.45, sky ink ≥
  0.005. Anything worse → block with `## Questions` (a failed orientation
  test most likely means `SLOPE_K` in T-0134 misclassifies; say so).
