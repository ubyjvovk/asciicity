## Goal
In the reference, light diffuses through wet air: distant lights bloom into
soft halos, the sky glows over the city, mist hangs low over the street.
Ours has only a flat distance fog. Implement the atmosphere: analytic height
fog, fog-weighted wide halos from the emissive layer, horizon light
pollution in the sky, and a drifting ground mist. Contract:
docs/architecture.md §4.11 "`cyberpunk` v2 (wave 20b)" → "Atmosphere" and
"Plumbing".

## Context
- `src/render/punk/atmosphere.ts` exists (PM split) with the upstream
  distance fog; keep the API: `createAtmosphere(i: AtmosphereInputs): Atmosphere`
  → `{ rgb, update(camera, timeS), stats() }`. `AtmosphereInputs` and
  `FogUniforms` shapes are PM-owned (do not change them); the pipeline
  (`pipeline.ts`, PM-owned) refreshes `cameraWorld` and
  `projectionInverse` every frame before `update`.
- World position of a pixel: `getViewPosition(screenUV, depth,
  projectionInverse)` (from 'three/tsl'), then `cameraWorld.mul(vec4(p, 1))`.
  Camera world position = `cameraWorld` column 3. Sky = linear depth > 0.98.
  y0 (camera ground height) = camera y − 1.7: keep your own `uniform` set in
  `update` from `camera.position.y`.
- Wide halo: `gaussianBlur(i.emissive, sigmaNode, 4, { resolutionScale: 0.25 })`
  from 'three/addons/tsl/display/GaussianBlurNode.js' (allowed in this
  directory), weight `0.25 + fogAmountAtPixel`. Horizon glow for sky
  pixels: the blurred emissive sampled along the horizon band (a few taps
  at the horizon row of the current column is enough), fading to 0 at 30°
  above the horizon (use the view ray's world y).
- Ground mist: `vnoiseNode` from `src/render/punk/noise.ts` (PM-owned),
  2 octaves, drift 0.3 m/s via a time uniform, only within 2.5 m above y0,
  opacity ≤ 0.25, colour = the fog colour.
- Pure mirror `src/render/punk/fogmath.ts` (NO `three/webgpu` import):
  `heightFogDensity(y, rho0, H, y0)`, `heightFogTransmittance(camY, pY, dist,
  rho0, H, y0)` (closed form of ∫ρ along the straight segment; handle
  camY ≈ pY with the horizontal limit), `mistOpacity(y, y0, noise)`, and
  constants (H = 18, RHO0_BASE = 0.006, MIST_H = 2.5, MIST_MAX = 0.25).
  atmosphere.ts must use the same constants and formula.
- Keep total cost ≤ 1.5 ms at 1280×720 on a desktop GPU: one blur at ¼ res,
  no per-pixel loops > 8 taps.
- **Never open or read any `.png`/`.jpg`.**
