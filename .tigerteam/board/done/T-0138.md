## Goal
User review of v3: the sky reads as "a big wire dome" and "many surfaces
shimmer when walking". Both are v3 formula defects, fixed by the five
locked changes in `docs/architecture.md` §4.11, subsection "`scribble` v4
(T-0138)" — read it first; it is the contract: (1) per-pixel depth `dPix`
feeds the wall position too; (2) wobble and lifts in world metres with
screen-size fades, never `ma` in a phase; (3) soft layer switching;
(4) wider anti-aliasing; (5) short hair strokes on the sky dome replacing
the tangle.

## Context
- Edit `src/render/styles/scribble.ts` in place; keep the exported `STYLES`
  shape and every v3 export the spec does not delete. `tangleInk` is
  deleted; new pure exports: `wobble(along, ph, mu, ma)`,
  `lifted(key, along, ma): boolean`, `hairInk(ps: [x, y], density)`;
  `skyDensity` gets the new endpoints (0.75 / 0.45).
- The hair loop is 3 × 3 cells × 3 strokes = 27 segment evaluations per
  sky pixel — constant-bound loops, fine in GLSL ES 1.0. Sky pixels are
  the only ones that pay for it.
- Unit tests (`tests/styles/scribble.test.ts`): keep the v3 cases that
  still apply; delete the `tangleInk` cases; update `skyDensity`
  expectations; add, by name:
  1. `wobble` stability — for 50 random `(along, ph, mu)` samples, the
     value at `ma = 0.05` equals the value at `ma = 0.1` (toBeCloseTo, 9)
     (all three octaves fully visible for both);
  2. `wobble` fades — at `ma = 5` (every period < 3 cells) the value is
     0 for any input; at `ma = 1.2` only the 14 m octave contributes
     (value equals `mu · 0.3 · sin(2π·along/14 + 3ph)`, toBeCloseTo, 6);
  3. `lifted` — never true at `ma = 10` (nothing visible); over a 1000 m
     line at `ma = 0.05` the lifted fraction is between 0.05 and 0.25;
  4. `hairInk([x, y], 0)` → 0 on a 64 × 64 grid at 1-cell steps; every
     value at density 1 lies in `[0, 1]`; the grid mean is strictly
     increasing across densities `[0, 0.45, 0.75, 1]`, and at density 0.75
     the mean is between 0.05 and 0.30;
  5. hair points up — for 200 random `ps` with `|ps| > 20`, the stroke
     direction `dir` computed for that point's own cell and stroke 0 has
     `dot(dir, normalize(−ps)) > 0.8` (export a small helper
     `hairDir(c: [cx, cy], s): [x, y]` for this).
- `docs/styles/scribble.md`: rewrite the wobble/lift step, the sky step
  (hair), the pure-exports list.
- **Never read, open or view any `.png`**; `ls -la` only. The PM does the
  visual review (two frames one step apart, and a sky pan).

## Questions

Two locked v4 formulas make the unchanged T-0135 e2e impossible. I did not guess a different mix or wobble.

**1. Sky mix 0.7 vs e2e ink (`max < 0.35`).**  
`mix(PAPER, SKY_INK, 0.7)` at coverage 1 is `(0.399, 0.389, 0.408)`. e2e ink requires max < 0.35, so **no sky pixel can count as ink**. Need a mix factor ≥ 0.759 (`0.98 − 0.83t < 0.35`). Night aimed skyInk 0.0055 is leftover building silhouette, not hair. Architecture also says “expect ≈ 10–15 % ink in open sky”, which I read as coverage, not the e2e metric.

Options:
1. Raise the sky mix to **0.85** (v3) or **0.80** in §4.11 — one-line spec fix; hair cores become e2e-ink. (Recommended if e2e must stay byte-identical.)
2. Keep 0.7 and amend `e2e/scribble.spec.ts` (out of this ticket’s scope): e.g. sky ink = distance from PAPER, or `INK_MAX` 0.45 for the aimed sky crop.
3. Darken `SKY_INK` so that mix 0.7 still dips below 0.35 — changes the look more than (1).

**2. Ground 6 px run-count vs world-metre wobble.**  
v3 (same scene, Swiftshader): groundHorizRuns 730 / vert 456. v4 spec: 456 / 456. Vert 456 is the pre-existing building-base lattice (unchanged since v3). The extra ~270 horizontal runs were the constant-depth ground strokes; world-metre wobble (0.9/3.5/14 m, amplitude up to 1.1 cells, all octaves fully visible on the near ground where `ma ≈ 0.02` m/cell) plus 1.5 m lifts stop those strokes forming ≥ 6 consecutive dark pixels. This is the feature v4 asked for (stable world-metre wobble), measured by a metric that assumed v3’s slow screen-space phase.

Options:
1. Keep the shader; amend e2e assertion 5 (factor 1.5, or drop it for v4). (Recommended.)
2. Soften the 0.9 m octave (drop it, or cut `A_0`) so near-ground strokes stay flatter on screen — a new design, not in the ticket.
3. Keep v3 `along/ma` frequency but fade octaves with `vis` — would pass e2e but reintroduces the writhing v4 is supposed to kill.

I will not pick a mix factor or a wobble rewrite without a spec amendment. Unit tests, docs, and the shader stay as §4.11 v4.

## PM answer 2 (2026-09-18) — I looked at the frames; four amendments, all on `main` §4.11 v4

The hair sky is right (no more cage). What is wrong is mine: sine wobble
at a fixed world period corrugates every stroke the same way and adjacent
ground lines wave in step, which prints moiré ribbons on the far road;
and the 0.45 core makes the 2-cell layer near-solid. Changes:

1. **Hand tremor = per-line value noise, not sine** (`vnoise1` new pure export):
       vnoise1(x, key) = mix(hash2(floor(x), key), hash2(floor(x) + 1, key), smoothstep(0, 1, fract(x)))
       wob = mu · Σ_{k=0..2} A_k · (2 · vnoise1(along / P_k + 3·ph, key + 11·k) − 1) · vis(P_k)
             P = [0.9, 3.5, 14] m,  A = [0.35, 0.30, 0.30]
   Unit case 1 (stability across `ma`) and case 2 (fades) stay, with the
   sine expectation in case 2 replaced by the vnoise term for the 14 m
   octave; add `vnoise1` continuity: |vnoise1(x + 1e−4, k) − vnoise1(x, k)| < 1e−2 at 100 random x.
2. **Core** `hw = mu · (0.38 + 0.15 · tone)`, edge `± 0.22·mu`.
3. **Hairs** longer and thinner: `len = H · (0.9 + 2.0 · r_5)`, `cov = (1 − smoothstep(0.15, 0.40, |s⊥ − bend|)) · endF`.
