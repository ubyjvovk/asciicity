### Derivation (T-0098 format)

Method: build a naive `blocked()` from the 3×3 tiles centred on each
station-anchor's tile, scan every road vertex in those tiles, filter by
distance to the anchor and by walkability at ≥ 2 m clearance, then choose
the vertex closest to the intended focal (geographic Scramble Crossing /
just-east-of-Shinjuku), unproject to WGS84 using the codebase's
`M_PER_DEG_LON_EQ = 111320` / `M_PER_DEG_LAT = 110574` (a first pass with
`R π / 180 = 111 195` produced a 129.8 m round-trip distance that failed
the ticket's 120 m cap — fixed by round-tripping through `project()`'s
exact constants). Walkability then re-verified via the actual
`CollisionGrid` in the new unit tests (both survive plain, 2 m and 6 m
clearance checks).
