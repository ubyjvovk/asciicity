## Goal
Buildings are flat-topped prisms. Render the OSM roof data (`Building.roof`,
already in the schema) as real roofs in every style, and carry the OSM
facade colour / roof colour / material to the GPU as the `extra` vertex
attribute for the cyberpunk facades. The algorithm is LOCKED in
docs/architecture.md §4.2, paragraph "Roofs + OSM facade data (wave 21,
`buildings.ts`)" — read it first, and data-format "Simple 3D Buildings".

## Context
- `src/world/buildings.ts`: `buildBuildingsMesh` (walls group 0, roofs
  group 1), `emitWall`, `emitRoof` (triangulates with
  `THREE.ShapeUtils.triangulateShape`), `ringHeights`, `normalizeRing`,
  landmark caps (`shape`: dome/spire/tower) which WIN over `roof`.
- `MeshBuilder.setExtra(v)` / `EXTRA_NONE` in `src/world/mesh.ts`
  (PM-owned, landed): call `setExtra` ONLY for buildings that have
  `osmColor`, `roofColor` or `material` — a dataset without them must
  produce NO `extra` attribute and byte-identical geometry (existing tests
  check London/synthetic output; keep them green unchanged).
- sRGB → linear for the colour channels: `c ≤ 0.04045 ? c/12.92 : ((c+0.055)/1.055)^2.4`.
- Material codes: 0 none, 1 brick, 2 stone, 3 concrete, 4 glass, 5 metal,
  6 wood, 7 plaster. Export `MATERIAL_CODE` for T-0161.
- Keep it pure (node-testable). Performance: this runs on the main thread
  for every streamed tile.

**⚠ Doc deviation, please review.** The §4.2 mesh recipe ("densify the ring to ≤ 2 m, then refine interior edges > 4 m at midpoints, 4 passes") is **not** implemented as written. There are two reasons:
- Budget: measured on london tile 0_0 (499 buildings, 3 m gabled roof on each), the text recipe gives 365 602 triangles against 20 290 flat, far over criterion 7.
- Exactness: a 10 m gable end split into 2 m segments has no vertex at t = 0, so criterion 1 ("gable walls reach base+15 at their midpoint ± 1e-4") cannot hold.

The shipped approach is exact for every shape except round:
- The ring is split where edges cross *active* crease lines (ridge, hips, pyramid diagonals, and 7 facet lines for round).
- It is triangulated with the same `ShapeUtils` triangulator `emitRoof` uses.
- Triangles are then cut along the crease lines, with crossings cached per undirected edge so there are no T-junctions. Boundary edges are never split, so walls share every roof boundary vertex.
- Result: 26 179 triangles, under the bound of 1.25·20 290 + 2 082 wall overhead = 27 445.
- Round is an 8-facet barrel approximation.

The full table, and the per-shape ratios across all london tiles, are in `docs/world.md`. The PM may want to update the §4.2 text to match (it is PM-owned, so I did not touch it).
