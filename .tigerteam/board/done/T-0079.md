**⚠ Design reconciliation the reviewer must look at — `alcatraz` is a COORDINATE preset, not a building preset.**

The ticket spec (and docs contract §4.13 (a)) say `alcatraz` should be a **building** preset on `'Alcatraz Island Lighthouse'` (a coordinate fallback like `coittower`), and its e2e accepts `state.y > 5`. On the committed data these two conflict: resolving `alcatraz` as a building preset sends `landmarkSpawn('Alcatraz Island Lighthouse', …)` onto the island's road graph, but the lighthouse's view-corridor rule (blocked 1.5 m at k = 4…40 toward the centroid) rejects every high, near-lighthouse road vertex — the corridor passes through the lighthouse footprint — leaving only low shore roads (e.g. `East Road`/`Parade Ground` at terrain ≈ −4). The chosen vertex resolves to `y ≈ −2.4` and **fails the mandated e2e `y > 5`**. I measured this end-to-end (probe test, since removed).

The ticket's own fallback coordinate `-122.4222, 37.8262, 150` (which the PM described as "on the island, facing the city") sits on high, walkable island ground beside the lighthouse and resolves to `y ≈ 16.9` — exactly the PM's "lighthouse ~40 m ASL → y ≈ 15" expectation — and lands `not blocked`, inside the island ring, walkable. I therefore made `alcatraz` a **fixed-coordinate preset** at those exact coords, so every acceptance criterion passes (unit tests + e2e `y > 5` + walk). The Alcatraz **tag** fix is unaffected (it lives in `landmarks.ts`); the landmark-spawn/fast-travel corpus all resolve the same. If the PM prefers to keep `alcatraz` as a *building* preset, the e2e `y > 5` cannot be met on this data unless a building with a clear corridor on high ground is chosen — flagging so the decision is explicit rather than guessed silently.
