## Goal
Replace the `scribble` stub with the real style: white paper, tone carried
by stroke density, long wobbly pen strokes that follow the surface
(vertical on walls, horizontal on the ground, a loose tangle in the sky),
ink coloured by the object under it, one-sided pencil outlines, far
buildings sketched lighter. Every formula is locked in
`docs/architecture.md` §4.11, subsection "`scribble` (wave 17, T-0134)" —
read it first; it is the contract. The stub already ships and is
registered (`STYLE_ORDER`, `styles/index.ts`; `R` cycles to it,
`?render=scribble` selects it, `groundGrid: false` is already declared).

## Context
- Rewrite `src/render/styles/scribble.ts` wholesale; keep the exported
  shape: `export const STYLES: readonly RenderStyle[]` with ONE entry,
  `id 'scribble'`, `label 'SCRIBBLE'`, `cellW 3`, `cellH 3`, `subX 1`,
  `subY 1`, `needsDepth: true`, `groundGrid: false`.
- The strokes are drawn **per canvas pixel in continuous cell space**
  (`p = vUv * grid`, NOT floored) — that is what makes a line run unbroken
  across cells. Only the scene sample, the depth taps and the class are
  per cell (`cell = floor(p)`).
- Patterns to copy: `styles/lowpoly.ts` lines ~250–260 (depth taps one
  CELL apart at texel centres — `texel = 1.0 / sceneSize`, `centreUv =
  (cell * sub + 0.5) * texel`, `stepUv = sub * texel`; do NOT sample one
  sub-sample apart like `edges.ts`), `styles/quest.ts` (one-sided outline
  via `isNearSide`, `daylight` uniform seeded in `makeUniforms` and copied
  in `update`), `styles/matrix.ts` (the `hash` function). Import `isEdge`
  from `./edges` and `isNearSide` from `./quest` for both the shader logic
  and the pure mirrors — do not re-implement them. Read
  `src/render/style.ts` for the prelude helpers; never redeclare prelude
  uniforms.
- The only style uniform is `daylight` (float, from
  `StyleContext.daylight`, refreshed in `update`). `makeUniforms` creates
  no textures; no `dispose` needed.
- GLSL: the three stroke layers and the three tangle families are
  constant-bound `for` loops (GLSL ES 1.0 unrolls them); keep the layer
  constants in `const float` arrays or literal `if (k == 0)` ladders —
  either is fine. Use `smoothstep` exactly where the spec does.
- Pure exports (one-line JSDoc each), mirroring the shader term for term:
  `SCRIBBLE_LAYERS` (3 × `{ spacing, threshold }`), `SLOPE_K`, `PAPER`,
  `INK`, `SKY_INK` (`[r, g, b]`), `hash2(a, b)`, `surfaceClass(dC, dU, dD,
  far): 0 | 1 | 2`, `depthFade(d)`, `strokeInk(u, along, tone)`,
  `tangleInk(px, py, density)`, `inkColour(tint)`, `washColour(tint, tone)`.
- New unit file `tests/styles/scribble.test.ts` (copy the shape of
  `tests/styles/quest.test.ts`). Cases, by name:
  1. `hash2` is deterministic and in `[0, 1)` for 200 integer pairs;
  2. `surfaceClass(1960, 1970, 1950, 2000)` → 0 (sky, `≥ 0.98·far`);
     `surfaceClass(20, 20.3, 19.7, 2000)` → 1 (ground, slope 0.015);
     `surfaceClass(20, 20.01, 19.99, 2000)` → 2 (wall, slope 0.0005);
     `surfaceClass(50, 50, 50, 2000)` → 2;
  3. `depthFade(0)` → 1; `depthFade(120)` → 1; `depthFade(900)` → 0.55;
     `depthFade(2000)` → 0.55 (toBeCloseTo, 6);
  4. `strokeInk(u, along, 0)` → 0 for a 64 × 64 grid of `(u, along)` at
     0.5 steps; every value of `strokeInk` on that grid at tone 0.5 and 1
     lies in `[0, 1]`;
  5. stroke density grows with tone: the mean of `strokeInk` over the
     same grid is strictly increasing across tones `[0, 0.2, 0.5, 0.8,
     1.0]`, and at tone 1 the mean is ≥ 0.25;
  6. `tangleInk(px, py, 0)` → 0 on a 64 × 64 grid at 1-cell steps; the
     mean of `tangleInk` over the grid is strictly increasing across
     densities `[0, 0.25, 0.55, 1.0]`;
  7. `inkColour([0.8, 0.8, 0.8])` equals `INK` (toBeCloseTo, 6);
     `inkColour([1, 0.2, 0.2])` has `r > g` and `r > b` and `r ≈ 0.55`;
     `washColour([1, 0.2, 0.2], 0)` equals `PAPER`; `washColour([1, 0.2,
     0.2], 1)` has `r > b`.
- New doc `docs/styles/scribble.md` — same shape as `docs/styles/quest.md`:
  cell/sub/depth line, "Algorithm (per pixel)" steps (class, fade,
  strokes, tangle, outline, colour), shader notes, pure-exports list,
  uniforms table.
