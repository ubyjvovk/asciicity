## Goal
Make Tokyo playable: registry entry, spawn presets resolved FROM THE
COMMITTED DATASET (never hand-typed coordinates), landmark height/colour
entries for the two towers, and a Tokyo e2e spec.

## Context
- **Read first**: architecture.md §4.19 (registry shape — copy the SF/T-0095
  entry), §4.13 (landmark table format + `applyLandmarks`), the preset
  machinery in `src/data/spawn.ts` (`presetsFor`, `landmarkSpawn`,
  `resolveSpawn`), and data-format.md "Central Tokyo".
- Registry: `id: 'tokyo'`, `label: 'TOKYO'`, `file: 'data/tokyo/index.json'`,
  `tiled: true`, `sizeBytes` = `stat -c %s` of the index, blurb like
  `'Imperial Palace to the Skytree · streamed'` (picker order: after
  Manhattan).
- Presets (names locked; coordinates are YOURS to derive from the data):
  `skytree`, `tokyotower`, `tokyostation`, `ginza`, `akihabara`,
  `imperialpalace`, `sumida` (a riverside vantage that faces the Skytree
  across/along the Sumida). Default spawn: `tokyostation`.
  Derivation rule (this is the T-0047 lesson — hand-typed coordinates put
  presets in rivers): read `public/data/tokyo/index.json` + the relevant
  tile files in node, pick a road vertex (or landmark anchor from
  `index.landmarks`) near the target, choose bearing to FACE the landmark,
  and prove every preset walkable with a unit test that builds a
  `CollisionGrid` from the preset's 3×3 tiles (fs-read in node) and
  asserts `blocked(preset) === false` and ground sanity. Tower presets are
  street vantages LOOKING AT the tower, not points inside its footprint.
- Landmark table (`src/world/landmarks.ts`, per-city section like
  Manhattan's): Tokyo Skytree → `shape: 'tower'` (h comes from data,
  ≈ 634 — do not override height unless the data is wrong; report if so);
  Tokyo Tower → `shape: 'tower'`, colour international orange `0xc0362c`
  (the GGB constant), h from data ≈ 333. Use the exact building names you
  find in `index.landmarks` (T-0097's report lists them) — add further
  entries only if the data shows a landmark rendering absurdly (flag in
  report).
- Names in HUD/tags will be `name:en` where OSM has it, else Japanese —
  both render; never transliterate (AGENTS.md).
