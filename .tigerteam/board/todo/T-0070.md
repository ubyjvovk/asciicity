## Goal
At `?city=london&at=trafalgar` both floating tags read "Trafalgar Square":
the 52 m extra shares the OSM name "Nelson's Column", so `landmarkAnchors`
resolves its label through the fix table (`label: 'Trafalgar Square'`) meant
for the plinth. Extras (ids ≤ −1000) must be tagged with their own `name`;
the fix's `label` applies to the OSM building only.

## Context
- `src/hud/tags.ts` `landmarkAnchors`: when `building.id <= -1000`, use
  `building.name` as the label regardless of the fix table.
- Test: a city with an OSM building `X` (fix `{ label: 'Plinth' }`) and an
  extra also named `X` (id −1000) yields two anchors labelled `Plinth` and
  `X` respectively.
- `docs/hud.md` tags section: one sentence on the rule.
