## Goal
Make the synthesised city selectable: `?city=minas-tirith` (and the picker)
boots the tiled dataset committed by T-0127 in the `quest` style at the
Great Gate, with `greatgate` / `citadel` / `healing` spawn presets, per
`docs/architecture.md` §4.23 "Registry / presets".

## Context
- `src/data/cities.ts`: append the `CityInfo` entry (`id 'minas-tirith'`,
  label `MINAS TIRITH`, `file 'data/minas-tirith/index.json'`,
  `defaultSpawn 'greatgate'`, blurb `Gondor's seven-tiered city ·
  synthesised`, `sizeBytes` = the committed index size from T-0127's
  report, `tiled: true`, `defaultRender: 'quest'`). Update the
  `defaultRender` JSDoc ("Only Tokyo sets one") to name both cities.

  `minas-tirith` has `defaultRender 'quest'`; the three presets resolve to
