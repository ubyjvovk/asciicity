## Goal
In the real dataset ~1/3 of buildings are named, so the minimap is a wall of
bright green. Make footprints dim and roads the bright element so the street
pattern reads at a glance.

## Context
- `src/hud/minimap.ts` (T-0014) `update()` draws roads then buildings.
- New colours: unnamed footprint `#143019`, named footprint `#245c2f`, roads
  `#3fb85a` at 1 px, player triangle and `N` marker unchanged (`#8aff9e`).
  Draw order becomes **buildings first, then roads on top** so roads are
  never hidden by footprints.
- Update the colour table in `docs/minimap.md`.
