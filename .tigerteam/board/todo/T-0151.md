## Goal
Pin the `retrocgi` look (docs/architecture.md §4.11 "`retrocgi` (wave 19
…)", shader landed by T-0149) with a Playwright spec that reads canvas
pixels, so a later change cannot silently turn it back into `edges` or
into a black screen.

## Context
- Pattern to copy: `e2e/lowpoly.spec.ts` — boot with `?synthetic=1`,
  `waitReady`, read pixels from the WebGL canvas (the renderer has
  `preserveDrawingBuffer: true`) into counts, assert on shares. Use
  `&cell=2x2&crt=0&hud=0&minimap=0&tags=0` and a 640×360 viewport so the
  read is cheap. Keep the SwiftShader note from that spec in mind (rows
  may blank below 64 cells; sample the region lowpoly.spec.ts samples).
- Import the constants from `src/render/styles/retrocgi.ts`
  (`RETRO_LINE`, `RETRO_BG`, `FLICKER`, `FADE_MIN`) — do not retype them.
- The line colour flickers ±4 % and fades to 35 % with distance and the
  halo is 0–30 %, so classify each pixel by SHAPE, not by exact value:
  `bg` = every channel ≤ 12/255; `green` = `g ≥ 40/255` and
  `g ≥ 1.8·r` and `g ≥ 1.5·b`; anything else = `other`.
- Assertions (one `test`, numbered comments):
  1. `body.dataset.render === 'retrocgi'` and `__asciicity.groundGrid === false`;
  2. `other` share ≤ 1 % (nothing but black and green — this is what
     separates it from `edges`, whose dim scene colours are `other`);
  3. `green` share between 3 % and 45 % (lines exist, screen not flooded);
  4. `bg` share ≥ 50 %;
  5. at least one pixel with `g ≥ 0.85·255·(1 − 2·FLICKER)` (a near,
     unfaded line);
  6. shoreline: boot a second page aimed at water. Find how
     `e2e/ships.spec.ts` or `e2e/sf.spec.ts` gets water into view in the
     synthetic city (or `?at=`), and assert that a frame whose lower half
     is water still has `green` share ≥ 0.5 % in that half. If the
     synthetic city has no water, say so in the Worker report and skip
     assertion 6 with `test.fixme` and a comment — do not invent data.
- Add an "E2E" section to `docs/styles/retrocgi.md`: the pixel classes and
  the six assertions, one line each.
- **Never read or open any `.png`**; do not add screenshots to the spec.
