## Goal

`src/data/types.ts`, rules in docs/data-format.md "Tokyo from PLATEAU"
items 2 and 6) in both `validateCity` and tile validation.

## Context
- Follow the style of the wave-21 `roof` validation (`validateRoof`) and
  `minH`: throw with a path like `buildings[i].tiers[k].h`.
- Rules: array of 1–64; each tier `h` finite in [1, building.h]; `poly` same
  rules as a building `poly` (≥ 3 finite points, …); `roof` and `tiers`
  together → error. Plan-disjointness is NOT validated (too expensive).
