## Goal
User: "Trafalgar Square looks wrong without a proper Nelson's Column". OSM
maps the column as its 338 m² plinth at the default 14 m. Per
`docs/architecture.md` §4.13 (London paragraph, 2026-08-27): fix the plinth
to 6 m (label `Trafalgar Square`), add a 5×5 m, 52 m ivory extra named
`Nelson's Column` at (−0.12793, 51.50776), and make the `trafalgar` preset
`{ building: "Nelson's Column", city: 'london', label: 'Trafalgar Square, facing Nelson\'s Column' }`
with the current coordinates kept as its fallback.

  `src/world/landmarks.ts` — same shapes as the Kyiv entries (`label` is

  clear per the T-0059 rule, facing it within 10°.

  preset row.
