- The 52 m extra (`landmarks.ts:175`) now stands on top of the real OSM column parts: shaft 14–46 m, statue/capital slab 1386955198, pedestal 1386955197.
- Separate converter note (existing rule, not T-0158): `minH` is kept only when `minH < h − 1`. So 1 m slabs with `min_height` are grounded, e.g. 1386955198 (`min_height 43, height 44`) becomes a solid 0–44 m block of 19 m², and 1386955197 (`12/13`) a solid 0–13 m block of 45 m². At Trafalgar that reads as a fat column, which is plausible, but elsewhere floating 1 m slabs will become full-height towers.
