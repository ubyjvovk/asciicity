## Goal
Give Manhattan's three East River suspension bridges the §4.16 structure.
The Brooklyn Bridge has ONE central promenade, so the spec grows a
one-walkway form with an explicit `deckWidth`. Contract:
`docs/architecture.md` §4.16 (`sidewalks: [string, string] | [string]`,
`deckWidth`, and the "One-walkway bridges (wave 10)" frame rule) — read it
first.

## Context
- `src/world/bridge.ts`: implement the one-walkway frame (walkway polyline
  = axis line straightened through the towers; `sep = deckWidth`); keep the
  two-walkway path byte-identical for SF (existing tests).
- `SUSPENSION_BRIDGES.nyc`: Brooklyn Bridge (towers 84 m above water; deck
  ≈ 41 m → `towerTopAboveDeck` ≈ 43; `deckWidth` 26; `sideSpan` 284;
  colour granite `0x8f857a`), Manhattan Bridge (towers 102 m; deck ≈ 41 →
  61; two walkways if both exist in nyc.json else one with `deckWidth` 37;
  `sideSpan` 221; steel blue-grey `0x6f7f8f`), Williamsburg Bridge (towers
  94 m; deck ≈ 40 → 54; walkway(s) from the data; `deckWidth` 36;
  `sideSpan` 180; `0x7a6f66`). Tower centres: query Overpass for the piers
  (`bridge:support=pier` / `man_made=tower` ways under each bridge, as the
  GGB's were found) and use the pylon centroids — record the OSM ids and
  coordinates in the report; never guess. Walkway names verbatim from
  nyc.json.
- `tests/bridge.test.ts`: `'one-walkway spec builds a deck deckWidth wide centred on the walkway'`
  (synthetic city), `'nyc bridges: tower centres lie within 3 m of their walkway lines'`,
  `'nyc bridge meshes have no NaN and stay under the vertex budget'`,
  `'SF bridge output is unchanged'` (vertex count and a few sampled
  positions equal to the pre-change values).
- `docs/integration.md`: NYC bridges note.
