## Goal
Replace the stub `src/render/styles/matrix.ts` with the real **matrix (digital rain over the city)** look,
exactly as specified in `docs/architecture.md` §4.11 "matrix". One file,
one shader, pure helpers unit-tested in node.

  helpers — `sampleSub`, `cellMean`, `bright`, `shaped`, `tintOf`,
  `linearDepth`) and `src/render/styles/ascii.ts` (the reference style:
  how the atlas, `makeUniforms` and the fragment fit together) before

  stub declares; keep cell/sub/needsDepth per §4.11.

- Import `buildGlyphAtlas`/`DEFAULT_RAMP` from `./ascii` for the atlas; the rain phase uses the common `time` uniform (no `update` hook needed).
- `docs/styles/matrix.md`: the algorithm in prose, the pure exports, and a
  note on what the shader does per pixel.
