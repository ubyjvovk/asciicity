## Goal
A fourth ascii-family look, `amber` (`R`-cycle position 4, `?render=amber`):
near-black frame, amber/gold glyphs with olive-green accents, crushed darks,
hot cells (lamps, lit windows, sun/moon) blooming warm white. The formula is
locked in `docs/architecture.md` §4.11, subsection "`amber` (wave 8)" — read
it first; it is the contract. The stub already ships
(`asciiStyle('amber', 'AMBER', 3)` currently renders the solarized branch).

## Context
- Only `ASCII_FRAGMENT` and the pure mirrors in `src/render/styles/ascii.ts`
  change: add the amber density (`aV`/`aDens`) and colour branch per §4.11,
  make the glyph index use the theme-selected density
  (`theme < 2.5 ? dens : aDens`), and keep the raw tint available for the
  amber branch (the existing in-place luminance fold feeds themes 0–2).
- HARD constraint: themes 0–2 stay pixel-identical. Do not edit `themeMix`,
  its tests (`tests/ascii.test.ts`), or any term of the existing branches —
  restructure only by introducing new locals.
- New pure exports mirroring the shader term-for-term (like
  `themeMix`/`glyphIndex` do): `amberDensity(v, gamma): number`,
  `amberMix(rawTint, v, mask, gamma): [r, g, b]`.
- New unit file `tests/styles/amber.test.ts` (do not touch
  `tests/ascii.test.ts`): amberDensity(0.06, 0.45) === 0 and
  amberDensity(1, 0.45) === 1, strictly monotone on a grid, matches
  `pow((v − 0.06)/0.94, 0.675)` at v = 0.5; amberMix: grey rawTint
  `[1,1,1]`, v 0.5, mask 1 → r > g > b (amber order); green rawTint
  `[0.3,1,0.3]` → g ≥ r (olive); v = 1 → every channel ≥ 0.5 (bloom);
  mask 0 → `[0,0,0]`.
- New e2e file `e2e/amber.spec.ts` (copy the boot helpers from
  `smoke.spec.ts`; never edit smoke.spec.ts). On
  `/?synthetic=1&render=amber&cell=3x6&time=23:00`, after ready + two
  double-rAF waits, compute in-page pixel stats exactly like the
  `nonBlackFraction` helper in `smoke.spec.ts` (offscreen 2d canvas +
  `drawImage(#view)` + `getImageData`) and assert:
  1. non-black fraction > 0.02 (it renders at all);
  2. dark floor: fraction of pixels with max(r,g,b) < 13/255 ≥ 0.35
     (crushed blacks);
  3. warm dominance: over pixels with max(r,g,b) > 38/255, mean(r) ≥
     1.4 × mean(b).
- The style loop in `smoke.spec.ts` already cycles 13 styles and saves
  `e2e/__shots__/style-amber.png` — that artifact is the PM's visual
  review; make sure it is generated by your final `check.sh` run.
- §4.11 says constants are a starting point: if a criterion fails, tune
  constants (black point, exponent, chroma endpoints) until criteria pass,
  and record the final constants in the Worker report. Do NOT edit
  `docs/architecture.md` (PM-owned) — report instead.
