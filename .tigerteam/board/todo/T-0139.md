## Goal
Move the stroke machinery of `scribble.ts` into a new shared module
`src/render/styles/strokes.ts` so the wave-18 sketch styles (`pencil`,
`crayon`, `pastel`, `watercolor`) can reuse it, with **no change in
behaviour**: the scribble frames must stay pixel-identical (the PM diffs
host screenshots before/after). Contract: `docs/architecture.md` §4.11,
subsection "Shared stroke chunk (T-0139)" — read it first.

## Context
- `strokes.ts` exports `STROKE_GLSL: string` — the GLSL helpers currently
  in `scribble.ts` (`hash2`, `viewPos`, `viewNormal`, `strokeCoords`,
  `strokeScale`, `wobble`, `lifted`, `strokeLayer`, `nestedStrokeInk`,
  `hairInk`, `skyCoords`, `depthFade`, plus the `Surf` struct and
  `Surf surfaceAt(vec2 uv)` that does everything scribble's `main()`
  computes before colouring: the five clamped taps, sky test, class,
  `dPix`, `W`, `nW`, `strokeCoords` + `strokeScale`, the one-sided
  outline test; for sky cells fill `dirW`/`ps`, zero the rest). The chunk
  must NOT redeclare prelude uniforms (`tScene`, `tDepth`, `viewUp`,
  `tanHalfFov`, `viewToWorld`, …) — it is prepended to a style's fragment
  which is itself appended to `STYLE_PRELUDE` (`src/render/style.ts`).
- `nestedStrokeInk` gains three width parameters:
  `nestedStrokeInk(u, along, tone, mu, ma, wBase, wTone, aa)` with
  `hw = mu·(wBase + wTone·tone)` and the edge `± aa·mu`; scribble passes
  `(0.45, 0.25, 0.25)` (its v4 values). Same for the TS mirror.
- The TS pure mirrors of everything in the chunk move to `strokes.ts`
  (same names, same JSDoc); `tests/styles/strokes.test.ts` takes their
  unit tests out of `tests/styles/scribble.test.ts` unchanged in
  substance. `scribble.ts` keeps `PAPER`, `INK`, `SKY_INK`, `UP_K` re-export
  or import, `skyDensity`, `inkColour`, `washColour`, its fragment
  `main()` (now `Surf s = surfaceAt(vUv);` first) and `STYLES` with
  `fragment: STROKE_GLSL + SCRIBBLE_FRAGMENT`.
- `docs/styles/strokes.md` (new): what the chunk provides, the `Surf`
  fields, how a style uses it (5 lines of example). `docs/styles/scribble.md`:
  point the machinery sections at strokes.md.
- Never open any `.png`; `ls -la` only.
