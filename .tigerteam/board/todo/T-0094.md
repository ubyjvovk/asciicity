## Goal
The runtime scheduling half of sector streaming: a pure `TileManager`
(no three.js, no DOM — plain node-testable TS) that decides which 1000-m
tiles to fetch/build/drop around the player, and `CollisionGrid` support
for adding/removing per-tile collision sources. No `main.ts` wiring yet
(that is T-0095) — this ticket is entirely unit-tested.

## Context
- **Read first**: architecture.md §4.19 (the complete runtime spec — API,
  radii, event semantics) and data-format.md "Tiled datasets" (the format).
  Types `TileIndexData`/`TileData` are in `src/data/types.ts` (PM-owned).
- Locked API (§4.19): constructor takes the index, an injected
  `loadTile(key) => Promise<TileData>`, and optional `{ loadR, unloadR }`
  (defaults 2000 / 2600). `update(x, z)`; `take(): TileEvent[]` — at most
  ONE `add` per call, any number of `remove`s; `snapshot()` with a
  `version` counter; `loadedKeys()`; `pending()`. A tile counts as loaded
  only after its `add` event has been TAKEN by the caller.
- Locked behaviour: wanted = tiles whose rect intersects the `loadR`
  circle; drop only when entirely outside `unloadR` (hysteresis); the
  player's tile + 8 neighbours are NEVER dropped regardless of radii;
  fetches scheduled nearest-first, ≤ 2 in flight; a failed `loadTile` is
  retried once, then `console.warn` + skipped (and may be wanted again on
  a later `update` — that is fine, but must not loop hot: re-eligible only
  after the tile leaves and re-enters the wanted set).
- `CollisionGrid` (src/world/collision.ts, §4.6): add
  `addSource(key: string, buildings: Building[], corridors: Corridor[]): void`
  and `removeSource(key: string): void`. The constructor's arguments form a
  permanent base source; water rings stay constructor-only (parity + shore
  semantics untouched). Public `blocked`/`resolve` signatures and behaviour
  are unchanged. Internal representation is yours — but removal must not
  degrade `blocked` to a scan (keep it bucketed).
- `snapshot()` concatenates buildings/roads of ADDED tiles only, is cached
  until the added-set changes, and bumps `version` on every change. A
  fetched-but-not-yet-taken tile is not in the snapshot.
- Async in tests: drive with manually resolved promises (deferred pattern)
  — no timers, no real fetch, fully deterministic.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `bash .tigerteam/scripts/run-tests.sh tests/tiles.test.ts tests/collision.test.ts` exits 0, and the full
      `bash .tigerteam/scripts/run-tests.sh` stays green (existing collision
      tests untouched and passing).
- [ ] `tests/tiles.test.ts` covers, by name: (1) at rest with default radii
      the wanted set is the 5×5 around the player's tile; (2) walking to just
      past a tile boundary and back does NOT unload anything (hysteresis);
      (3) the player tile + 8 neighbours survive even with
      `{ loadR: 1, unloadR: 2 }`; (4) fetches are issued nearest-first with
      never more than 2 unresolved `loadTile` calls; (5) `take()` returns at
      most one `add` per call while queued builds exist, and `remove`s flush
      immediately; (6) a tile is in `snapshot()`/`loadedKeys()` only after
      its `add` was taken; (7) `version` increments exactly once per
      added-set change and `snapshot()` is reference-stable between changes;
      (8) a rejected `loadTile` is retried once, then skipped with a
      `console.warn`, `pending()` returns to 0, and it is not re-requested
      until it leaves and re-enters the wanted set; (9) keys with negative
      indices (`"-1_0"`) round-trip through the whole flow; (10) tiles
      listed in the index but absent from the wanted circle are never
      fetched.
- [ ] `tests/collision.test.ts` gains: `blocked` true after `addSource`,
      false after `removeSource`; base-source footprints unaffected by
      `removeSource` of another key; corridors added via a source override
      footprints and water exactly as constructor corridors do; water parity
      (island-in-bay) behaviour identical with sources present; re-adding a
      removed key works.
- [ ] `src/world/tiles.ts` imports nothing from `three`, `document`, or
      `window` (pure data + promises).
- [ ] Every exported symbol has a one-line JSDoc; `docs/streaming.md` (new)
      explains the lifecycle (wanted → fetching → fetched → added →
      removed), the radii/hysteresis, and how `?tileradius` maps to
      `{ loadR, unloadR }` scaling.
- [ ] No files changed outside scope.
