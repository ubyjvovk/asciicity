## Goal
User review: the sketch styles "still have this blocky feeling from
ascii-like renders" and pastel must not have sharp edges. Two locked
changes in `docs/architecture.md` §4.11, subsection "Sketch family
resolution + soft pastel (wave 18b, T-0145)" — read it first; it is the
contract: (1) the five painterly styles (`scribble`, `pencil`, `crayon`,
`pastel`, `watercolor`) go to cell 2×2 with `targetCap: { w: 960, h: 540 }`
(the contract field is already in `src/render/style.ts`; `post.ts` and
the budget unit test already honour it); (2) `pastel` gets a per-pixel
bilinear blend of the 3×3-smoothed samples, a chalk-smudge jitter of the
sample position from `anchoredNoise` (T-0144), and a soft 2-cell edge
ramp instead of the binary cell outline, via a new chunk helper
`outlineAt(cellUv)` extracted from `surfaceAt`.

## Context
- Change (1) is five one-line edits to the `STYLES` entries (`cellW`,
  `cellH`, `targetCap`) plus the docs table rows in `docs/render.md`
  (cell column 3×3 → 2×2 for the five; mention the cap). Do not touch
  the shaders for (1).
- Change (2): `strokes.ts` — extract the one-sided outline test into
  `float outlineAt(vec2 cellUv)` and call it from `surfaceAt` (byte-identical
  behaviour for every other style; the scribble e2e proves it). `pastel.ts`
  — implement the spec's per-pixel blend, jitter and soft edge; pure
  exports `bilinearWeights(p)`, `softEdge(e)`.
- Unit tests: `tests/styles/pastel.test.ts` adds, by name: (1)
  `bilinearWeights` sums to 1 and is `[1,0,0,0]` at a cell centre
  (`p − 0.5` integer), `[0.25 ×4]` at a cell corner; (2) `softEdge(0)` → 0,
  `softEdge(1)` → 0.35, monotone. `tests/styles/strokes.test.ts` keeps
  passing unchanged.
- Docs: `docs/styles/pastel.md` (new steps), `docs/styles/strokes.md`
  (`outlineAt`), `docs/render.md` rows.
- Never open any `.png`; `ls -la` only. The PM does the visual review
  and the fps check on the host GPU.
