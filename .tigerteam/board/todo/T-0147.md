## Goal
User review of the sketch styles: the hair sky and the lines on every
horizontal surface must go; a good quick sketch is mostly white paper,
contrasting edges, soft shading here and there, no uniform thatch, one
stroke direction per area. The six rules are locked in
`docs/architecture.md` §4.11, subsection "Pencil / crayon v2 — quick
drawing (wave 18c, T-0147)" — read it first; it is the contract.

## Context
- `pencil.ts`: rewrite `main()` around the six rules; the stroke /
  shading block must be byte-identical in `crayon.ts` (only the colour
  lines differ: crayon's `strokeCol`, wash colour `mix(G, tint, satF)`).
  Both skies are plain `PAPER_P` now.
- Helpers already in the chunk (`strokes.ts`, read `docs/styles/strokes.md`):
  `surfaceAt`, `outlineAt(cellUv)`, `toothOf`, `nestedStrokeInk(...,
  wBase, wTone, aa)`, `depthFade`, `hash2`. The 3×3 mean of `sampleSub`
  is the same 9-tap loop `pastel.ts` uses — copy it. `crossCoords` stays
  in `pencil.ts`.
- The "neighbour is sky" test for the silhouette strength: read
  `linearDepth` at the four neighbour cell centres (one cell apart, as
  `surfaceAt` does) and compare with `0.98 · cameraFar`.
- Delete `groundTone` / `crossGate` and their tests (superseded). New
  pure exports with one-line JSDoc: `smudgeOf`, `wallWash`, `hatchGate`,
  `facadeDir`; unit cases as the spec lists, by name; crayon's test keeps
  asserting the re-export identity of the four.
- Docs: both style docs rewritten around the six rules.
- Never open any `.png`; `ls -la` only. The PM does the visual review.
