## Goal
Kyiv's two famous pedestrian bridges — the Parkovyi Bridge to Trukhaniv
Island (OSM way 163254636) and the Klitschko "glass" bridge (way 660559170,
name:en "Bridge over Volodymyrskyi Descent") — are tagged
`highway=cycleway` + `bridge=yes`, so the Overpass query never fetched them
and the `parkbridge` / `glassbridge` presets currently drop the player on the
river bank. Fetch cycleways, apply the existing footway-bridge rule to them,
regenerate `kyiv.json`, and place the two presets on the decks.

## Context
- `docs/data-format.md` (updated 2026-08-26): the Overpass highway regex now
  includes `cycleway`; the road table says footway **and cycleway** are
  dropped unless `bridge` ≠ `no`, in which case they become `pedestrian` +
  `bridge: true`. Implement exactly that in `scripts/fetch-osm.mjs`
  (`buildQuery`) and `scripts/osm-convert.mjs` (the T-0040 footway exception —
  extend it to `cycleway`; `roadClassOf('cycleway')` stays `null`).
- Fixture: add one `highway=cycleway` + `bridge=yes` way and one plain
  `highway=cycleway` way; tests: the bridged one → `pedestrian`/`bridge: true`
  named as in the fixture, the plain one dropped.
- Regenerate with `npm run fetch-data:kyiv` (network; ~1 min + DEM cache).
  Expect the two bridges to appear as `pedestrian` roads with `bridge: true`,
  ≈ 400 m (Parkovyi) and ≈ 200 m (Klitschko); counts otherwise within ±5 %
  of `8183 / 6740 / 193 / 51 / 19`. London `city.json` untouched.
- Presets (`src/data/spawn.ts`): set `parkbridge` and `glassbridge` to the
  **first vertex** of the respective bridge polyline (unproject it with
  `src/geo.ts` `unproject`, 5 dp) with `bearingDeg` = the bearing from that
  vertex to the last vertex (`atan2(dx, −dz)` in degrees, 0–360, rounded).
  Labels: "Parkovyi Bridge, facing Trukhaniv Island" and "Klitschko glass
  bridge, facing the Arch". Derive the numbers from the regenerated dataset
  and quote them in the report.
- Tests (`tests/spawn.test.ts`): for each of the two presets, `resolveSpawn`
  against the committed `kyiv.json` (with a `CollisionGrid` built the way
  `main.ts` builds it — water rings as footprints, bridge roads as corridors,
  see `src/main.ts`) returns a point that is **not blocked**, lies within
  3 m of a `bridge: true` `pedestrian` polyline, and stays unblocked 20 m
  further along its bearing (`resolve(from, to)` returns `to`).
- Docs: `docs/fetch-osm.md` (cycleway rule), `docs/integration.md` (the two
  preset rows).
