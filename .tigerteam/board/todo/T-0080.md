## Goal
The Golden Gate Bridge is currently a flat road across the water with two
misplaced thin boxes. Build the real structure from a spec table: two
towers (two legs each, portal arch the sidewalk passes through, four
struts), two main cables (parabola, 3 m deck clearance at mid-span, straight
side spans), hangers every 16 m, a 7.6 m truss deck box under the walkway,
anchorage blocks — all international orange. Contract: `docs/architecture.md`
§4.16 — read it in full first; every number, frame and rule there is the
spec. Also §4.13 "Wave 9 amendments" (b).
