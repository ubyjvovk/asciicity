## Goal
In the `lowpoly` style the sky currently posterises to a dark grey band at
every hour (the scene background and fog are black), so noon and midnight
look the same. Paint sky cells a flat '80s-CGI sky instead — navy at
night, pink at dusk/dawn, blue by day — driven by the new
`StyleContext.daylight` value. The formula is locked in
`docs/architecture.md` §4.11, subsection "`lowpoly` (wave 15, T-0120)",
paragraph "Sky cells (`cSky`, wave 15b / T-0122)" — read it first; it is
the contract.

## Context
- `StyleContext.daylight` (0 night … 1 day) already exists and is
  refreshed every 10 s (`src/render/style.ts`, `src/render/post.ts`,
  `daylightFactor` in `src/world/sky.ts`). Read it in `makeUniforms(ctx)`
  to seed `uniform float daylight`, and implement the optional
  `update(uniforms, timeS, ctx)` hook to copy `ctx.daylight` into that
  uniform every frame (see the `RenderStyle` interface for the hook shape;
  `styles/matrix.ts` implements `update` if you want an example).
- Shader change is small: after the existing edge test, if the cell is
  sky (`cSky`) and not an edge, output `LOWPOLY_SKY[band]` where
  `band = daylight < 0.33 ? 0 : (daylight < 0.66 ? 1 : 2)`. Use a
  `uniform vec3 lpSky[3]` filled from `LOWPOLY_SKY` like `lpHues`. Sky
  cells that ARE edges keep the ink colour (silhouettes stay outlined).
- New pure exports with one-line JSDoc, mirroring the shader:
  `LOWPOLY_SKY` (3 × `[r, g, b]` from `#10143C #E0508F #3A8CFF`, in that
  order) and `skyBand(daylight: number): number` (0, 1 or 2).
- `tests/styles/lowpoly.test.ts` — ADD cases (keep the existing ones
  untouched), by name:
  1. `LOWPOLY_SKY` has the three §4.11 colours in order;
  2. `skyBand`: 0 → 0, 0.32 → 0, 0.33 → 1, 0.5 → 1, 0.65 → 1, 0.66 → 2,
     1 → 2.
- `e2e/lowpoly.spec.ts` — the expected-colour set must now include the
  three `LOWPOLY_SKY` colours (import it), otherwise the palette-purity
  assertion fails once the sky is blue. Add two assertions to the existing
  test (or a second `test`), by name:
  5. at `time=12:00` the day-sky colour `LOWPOLY_SKY[2]` covers ≥ 0.05 of
     the pixels and the night colour `LOWPOLY_SKY[0]` covers < 0.005;
  6. booting `/?synthetic=1&render=lowpoly&cell=6x6&time=23:00` (same
     boot helpers), `LOWPOLY_SKY[0]` covers ≥ 0.05 and `LOWPOLY_SKY[2]`
     covers < 0.005.
  Keep assertions 1–4 and their thresholds exactly as they are.
- `docs/styles/lowpoly.md` — add the sky step to "Algorithm (per cell)"
  and `LOWPOLY_SKY`/`skyBand` to the pure exports list; mention the
  `daylight` uniform in the uniforms table.
- If your model cannot view images say so; the PM does the GPU review.
  Do NOT edit `docs/architecture.md`, `src/render/style.ts`,
  `src/render/post.ts` or `src/world/sky.ts` (PM-owned / already wired).
