## Goal
User: "water bodies aren't apparent on the minimap". Paint `city.water`
rings as a filled layer under buildings and roads, in a dim teal that reads
as water inside the green-on-black palette, and `city.rivers` as thin lines
of the same hue.

## Context
- `src/hud/minimap.ts` buckets buildings and road segments into a cell grid
  (`docs/minimap.md` §Cell grid) and draws in the order background →
  buildings → roads → player → north (§Colours). Add water rings to the same
  bucketing (bucket a ring into every cell its bounding box touches, like
  buildings) and river polylines like roads (`segs`), and draw them **first**
  after the background: water fill `#0e3a46`, rivers stroke `#155b6b`
  1 px. Ring projection: same heading-up transform as footprints.
- Large rings (the Dnipro is one 5 km² polygon) must not be re-projected
  per frame in full — clip is unnecessary; just draw the whole ring once
  per frame when any of its cells is in range (a `Set` of drawn ring ids per
  frame, as for buildings that span cells if such de-dup exists — mirror
  whatever `update` does for multi-cell buildings).
- Tests (`tests/minimap.test.ts`): a city with one water ring around the
  origin → after `update`, the pixel at the canvas centre (behind the arrow —
  sample a pixel 20 px from the centre) is `#0e3a46` (use the existing fake
  2D-context pattern in this test file, or `getImageData` if it runs on a
  real canvas); a ring 5 km away is not drawn (no water-colour fill calls);
  rivers produce one `stroke` in the river colour; cities without `water`
  are unchanged (same draw-call sequence as before).
- `docs/minimap.md`: update the draw order and colours sections.
