## Goal

review of T-0127's dataset: the White Tree at `(0, 0)` stands inside the
Tower Hall footprint, and the Rath Dínen tombs at radii 110–190 cross the
sixth wall (r 145). Corrected numbers are in `docs/architecture.md` §4.23
"Citadel and landmarks" (the T-0131 parentheses) — apply them, regenerate,
recommit the dataset, and update the registry size.
