## Goal
Ship Manhattan as the fourth city with twenty landmarks that read well:
real setback massing from building parts (T-0086), spire/tower/dome caps,
stone/glass colours, presets for each, and the Brooklyn Bridge deck as the
default spawn. Contracts: `docs/data-format.md` "### Manhattan" and
`docs/architecture.md` "§4.13 (wave 10) Manhattan landmarks" (the 20-row
table) — read both first. Precedents: T-0076 (SF dataset) and T-0079 in
`docs/integration.md`.
