## Goal
Draw `city.water` rings as a flat dark-blue surface just above the ground and
keep the player on land.

## Context
- Contract: `CityData.water?: Vec2[][]` (`src/data/types.ts`). Use the
  PM-owned `MeshBuilder`/`toGeometry` (`src/world/mesh.ts`) and
  `THREE.ShapeUtils.triangulateShape` exactly as roofs do in
  `src/world/buildings.ts` (read it: winding normalisation, upward winding).
- `src/world/water.ts` exports:
  `buildWaterMesh(rings: Vec2[][]): MeshData` (pure; y = `0.02`, normal
  `(0,1,0)`, uv `(0,0)`, colour `0x163a6b` linear via `THREE.Color`, one
  group 0; rings with < 3 points or |area| < 1 skipped) and
  `makeWaterObject(rings): THREE.Mesh` (`MeshBasicMaterial({ vertexColors: true })`).
- `main.ts`: when `city.water?.length`, add the water object to the scene and
  include the rings in collision as fake footprints:
  `new CollisionGrid([...city.buildings, ...city.water.map((poly, i) => ({ id: -1 - i, h: 1, poly }))])`.
  Keep everything else as is.
- Docs: `docs/world.md` (water section), `docs/integration.md` (startup
  sequence mentions water + collision).
- Verify visually with the throwaway Playwright recipe from T-0010 using
  `?at=tower` (looks south towards the river) and describe it.
