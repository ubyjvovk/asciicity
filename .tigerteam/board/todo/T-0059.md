## Goal
The user cannot find Kyiv's landmarks because OSM gives them stub heights
(Saint Sophia 3 m, Lavra bell tower 8 m, the Arch 3 m) and has no Motherland
Monument. Implement `docs/architecture.md` §4.13: a curated fix table
applied at load, an extra synthetic Motherland Monument, presets that
resolve to real buildings, exact-name matching and height-scaled spawn
distance, and `presetsFor(city)` for the coming fast-travel menu.
