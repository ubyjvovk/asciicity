## Goal
Make the cyberpunk streets look like the reference's wet asphalt: dark grainy
asphalt with patches, **distinct** puddles (25–35 % of road area) that act as
mirrors with rain ripples, damp asphalt between them, concrete pavement tiles
off-road, slope-darkened terrain, and water with slow waves. Today the whole
street is a uniform glitter. Contract: docs/architecture.md §4.11
"`cyberpunk` v2 (wave 20b)" → "Streets" (and "Plumbing").

## Context
- `src/render/punk/street.ts` exists (PM split) with the wave-20 look; keep
  the API exactly: `makeStreetMaterials(u: WetUniforms): StreetMaterials`
  → `{ road, ground, terrain, water }` (`MeshStandardNodeMaterial`s), assigned by
  `WetDressing` (`wet.ts`, PM-owned) to meshes tagged `userData.surface`
  `'road' | 'ground' | 'terrain' | 'water'`.
- Noise: ONLY `src/render/punk/noise.ts` (`fbm2Node`/`vnoiseNode` in TSL,
  `fbm2`/`vnoise` JS mirrors — same formulas). Ripples:
  `rippleNormal(u, scale, strength)` from `ripples.ts` (strength may be a node,
  e.g. `mix(damp, puddle, mask)`). Concrete tile seams: `bumpMap` from
  'three/tsl' on a height node, or perturb the normal.
- Puddle mask formula is locked: `smoothstep(0.54, 0.62, fbm2(x·0.07, z·0.07))`
  on world xz. Pure mirror `puddleAt(x, z)` in `streetmath.ts` (NO
  `three/webgpu` import) + `asphaltAlbedo(x, z)` and all constants, imported
  by street.ts so the shader and tests share numbers.
- Metalness drives the SSR reflection strength in `pipeline.ts`
  (PM-owned); puddles must be metalness 0.9 / roughness 0.03 so they mirror
  the neon; dry asphalt 0.12 / 0.45–0.6 so it does not.
- If the locked mask does not hit 25–35 % coverage, do NOT silently change
  the thresholds: report the measured coverage and block with a question.
- **Never open or read any `.png`/`.jpg`**; the PM does the GPU visual review
  and will rework "uniform glitter".
